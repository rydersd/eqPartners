pam-mdf-claims.html

PAM Portal · Alex Rivera · MDF Claim Review

Job to Be Done

"When a partner submits proof-of-spend for an approved MDF activity, I want to verify the claim against the original approval — so I can release reimbursement accurately and maintain a clean audit trail."

Screen Purpose

The single-claim review screen. Alex arrives here from the MDF Queue after selecting a submitted claim. She works through the checklist, reviews proof docs, and releases or rejects the reimbursement. The audit log at right captures every action automatically.

Key Design Decisions

  • Verification checklist as the primary affordance — Alex needs a structured review process, not just a form; the checklist makes the audit trail explicit and prevents skipping steps
  • Claimed vs. approved discrepancy calculated and displayed — under-claims (partner spent less) auto-adjust payout to claimed amount; over-claims block approval
  • Proof documents shown inline — Alex can verify invoice amounts directly without downloading files; the proof item component shows filename, size, date, and key metadata
  • ROI outcomes surfaced from partner-submitted data — Alex is judged on MDF program ROI; seeing the numbers in the claim record makes the program's value visible
  • Partner balance shown in right rail — ensures Alex always knows the financial context of the partner while processing a specific claim
  • PAM notes field — optional but important; any rejection should include a reason that routes back to the partner

Business Logic

  • Payout amount = MIN(Approved_Amount__c, Claimed_Amount__c)
  • Over-claims (Claimed > Approved) must be flagged and cannot be released without PAM override
  • Proof required when Approved_Amount__c > $5K (all tiers) or when Approval_Type = Pre-approval (all amounts)
  • Releasing reimbursement: updates MDF_Allocation__c.Spent_Amount__c, creates Reimbursement_Payment__c record
  • Rejected claims: sets Status = Rejected, logs rejection_reason, notifies partner portal
  • Audit trail: every open, checklist tick, and decision logged to Claim_Audit_Log__c
