Design Notes

Summary

Central repository for partner-facing sales playbooks, battle cards, and marketing collateral organized by use case and product. Enables partners to quickly find relevant sales enablement materials with search and filtering.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Access curated sales and marketing guidance
  • Find relevant playbooks for specific customer scenarios
  • Equip reps with Equinix-approved messaging and positioning
  • Support consistent sales methodology across team
  • Provide competitive positioning guidance

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Sales and marketing playbook library organized by use case and product
  • Playbook cards with title, description, type badge, and download count
  • Sections for Hybrid Cloud, Edge Computing, Disaster Recovery solutions
  • Product datasheets and technical resources card grid
  • Proposal templates and email library collection
  • Recently Added section highlighting new materials
  • Search bar and multi-criteria filters (Use Case, Product, Content Type)

Interactive Elements

  • Search playbooks by keyword
  • Filter by use case, product, content type
  • Download collateral directly from card
  • View download count and material metadata
  • Browse by category tabs and sections

Functionality

  • Search & Discovery
    • Index and expose Playbook__c records via search and filter criteria for quick access
    • Auto-tag playbooks by Use_Case__c and Product__c for filtering and categorization
  • Sorting & Display
    • Sort Recently Added by CreatedDate descending to highlight newest materials
    • Populate download count from File object attach records for usage visibility
  • Analytics & Tracking
    • Track collateral downloads and usage analytics for performance measurement and optimization
