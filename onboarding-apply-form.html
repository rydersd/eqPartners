Design Notes

Summary

Partner Organization Registration — Variant B (manual entry, no company search). Pre-wizard partner type gate (Step 0) blocks entry until user selects Lead Referral or Reseller. If unsure, user is linked to the standalone Partner Type Guide (onboarding-partner-type.html). Once past the gate, a 4-step wizard runs: Company & You → Industry → Business → Review. Companion: onboarding-apply-wizard.html = Variant A.

Figma Source

  • File: Partner-Central
  • Node: 2311-32822

Flow Position

  • Flow: Partner Organization Registration & Onboarding — Step 1 (Apply)
  • On submit: → onboarding-agreement-confirmation.html
  • Difference from Variant A: No Salesforce Account lookup. Higher risk of duplicate Account creation — recommend Variant A where possible.

Design Notes

  • All field rules identical to Variant A: options < 10 = checkbox/radio grid; ≥ 10 = select
  • Variant B is the fallback / simpler path — prefer Variant A in production to avoid duplicate Salesforce records
  • Both variants submit to the same confirmation flow
