Design Specs

Summary

Provides PSM a complete financial and relationship view of partner account: MRR growth trajectory, active deal pipeline by rep, training health as signal for deal readiness, and tier advancement framed around revenue + certification requirements (not just training metrics).

Jobs to Be Done

  • PSM inspects individual partner account to understand financial health and next actions
  • Tier Advancement card shows: "Gold $180K → Platinum requires $190K MRR + Pepper Potts' Advanced Security cert. S.H.I.E.L.D. deal will close the gap."
  • Deal Pipeline table shows 5 active deals, staged by probability, highlighting which deals are "Near Win" and which are "At Risk"
  • Rep Performance grid shows each rep's training + deal activity, flagging training gaps (Sarah Connor overdue cert despite owning $95K TCV deal)
  • Activity Timeline focuses on deal milestones, cert expirations, and stalled deals — not just enrollment events
  • Financial Summary sidebar shows MRR, pipeline value, commission pending, JBP attainment, and MDF burn
  • Near-Win alert directly links to S.H.I.E.L.D. Secure Connect deal with close date and revenue impact
  • Drives PSM action: "Get Pepper Potts' Advanced Security cert done so S.H.I.E.L.D. closes and we hit Platinum"

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Record header with Account name, Tier badge, Region, Partner Since date, PSM Assigned, Current MRR, Pipeline MRR, Near Tier indicator
  • Contextual alert banner showing primary blocker: "S.H.I.E.L.D. deal closes $10K gap to Platinum; Pepper Potts cert is final training blocker"
  • Tier Advancement Progress card showing Gold → Platinum with MRR threshold as primary blocker, cert pending status, and team certs summary
  • Deal Pipeline table showing 5 active deals with stage, value, probability, protection expiry, and near-win/at-risk status badges
  • Rep Performance grid showing each rep's role, cert count, active deals, deal value, and training status badge (On Track/Attention/Overdue)
  • Left sidebar Activity Timeline focused on deal registration, deal stage changes, and cert expirations
  • Right sidebar Financial Summary card with MRR, pipeline, QoQ growth, commission pending, JBP attainment, MDF remaining
  • Partner Details card with PSM, Partner Manager, primary contact, phone, industry, employee count

Interactive Elements

  • Encourage Partner Manager primary action button with modal workflow (links to S.H.I.E.L.D. deal and Pepper cert gap)
  • Share Tier Summary, Contact Manager, and View Report buttons
  • Deal name links in pipeline table navigate to Deal Record detail
  • Rep performance cards show inline training status badges
  • Alert banner contextual action links to near-win deal with close date
  • Financial metrics link to JBP performance and MDF usage breakdown

Functionality

  • Record Data Loading
    • Loads account record data via Account object: Partner_Tier__c (Gold), Current_MRR__c ($180K), Pipeline_MRR__c ($97K), Assigned_PSM__c (Marcus Chen)
    • Retrieves Opportunity records linked to Account with Stage, StageName, Amount, Probability, CloseDate; calculates pipeline total and near-term deals
    • Joins Contact records (reps) with their assigned Opportunities and training completion status
  • Tier Status Calculation
    • Evaluates Gold → Platinum advancement: compares Current_MRR__c against $190K threshold; identifies Platinum_MRR_Gap__c
    • Retrieves Opportunity Amount and Probability to calculate if any single deal closes gap (e.g., S.H.I.E.L.D. at $45K and 85%)
    • Checks Partner Manager (Pepper Potts) certification status against Platinum requirement (Advanced Security); flags as pending blocker
    • Triggers alert with specific deal name and cert requirement driving next action
  • Deal Pipeline Aggregation & Display
    • Populates Deal Pipeline table with all Opportunities where Account matches CloudBridge Solutions, sorted by Probability descending
    • Calculates probability status: >80% = Near Win, <50% = At Risk, 50-80% = On Track
    • Shows protection expiry from Opportunity.Expiration_Date__c (e.g., Mar 15 for S.H.I.E.L.D.)
    • Links rep via Opportunity.Owner field to show who owns each deal
  • Rep Performance Grid & Training Health
    • Aggregates Contact records by Role; counts completed/pending certs from training enrollment records
    • Calculates training badge status: completion_percent >= 80% = On Track, 50-80% = Attention, <50% or expired certs = Overdue
    • Counts active Opportunity records (Stage != Closed Won/Lost) per rep; sums Amount for deal value display
    • Highlights deal ownership mismatches (e.g., Sarah Connor 50% trained but owns $95K TCV deal)
  • Activity Timeline & Recommendations
    • Activity Timeline queries Opportunity creation/stage change events and Contact training enrollment/expiration events
    • Surfaces deal-critical activities: "S.H.I.E.L.D. registered", "Hammer Industries stalled", cert expirations blocking tier progress
    • Financial Summary calculates: QoQ_MRR_Growth from historical MRR trending; Commission_Pending = Current_MRR * Commission_Rate (15%); JBP_Attainment from Account YTD deals
