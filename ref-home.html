Design Notes — ref-home.html

Purpose

This is the post-onboarding home for a Lead Referral partner — a persona that previously had no surface after completing onboarding-apply-referral.html. They are not a reseller (no deal registration, no MDF, no tier). Their entire relationship with Equinix is: send warm leads, track them, collect commission.

Key Differences from Reseller (pm-) Surface

  • No deal registration (they submit a "lead," not a "deal")
  • No MDF access (referral partners don't receive co-marketing funds)
  • No tier/badge system (flat referral structure)
  • No training requirements (though resources are available)
  • Visibility is limited — Jamie can see stage (Qualifying / In Evaluation / Won) but not deal details or pricing
  • Equinix AE owns the sales motion; Jamie provides the warm intro and stays out of the process

New Persona: Jamie Torres

Meridian Consulting Group — a management consulting firm. Not a tech reseller; a business consultant whose clients sometimes need colocation or interconnection infrastructure. Jamie refers those conversations to Equinix rather than trying to resell the product, in exchange for a 2% commission on first-year ARR.

This persona type is common in professional services (consulting, legal, accounting) adjacent to enterprise IT decisions. Per Channeltivity's 2025 PRM guide, referral partners are among the fastest-growing partner types and the most underserved by portal UX.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Referral-1: Submit a warm lead quickly without navigating a complex deal registration form
  • Referral-2: Know whether a submitted lead has been picked up by Equinix and what stage it's in
  • Referral-3: Track commissions owed and paid without calling Equinix finance

Design Decisions

Quick submit form on the homepage

The most important action is submitting a lead. Burying it behind a nav link would replicate the UX failure of most partner portals. The form is right on the home screen — short, focused, no unnecessary fields.

Stage visibility without deal detail

Jamie can see "In Evaluation" but not the deal size, pricing, or correspondence. This is intentional — the customer relationship is between Equinix and the prospect; referral partners are not co-sellers. The pipeline table shows enough to answer "did anyone pick this up?" without leaking confidential deal information.

90-day stale marker

Pacific Rim Systems shows "Stale — 90d". This matters because the referral protection window is 90 days. After that, Jamie can resubmit with updated context. Without this indicator, the lead just disappears and Jamie doesn't know if it's dead or still in play.

Gaps / Open Questions

  • [?] Does Jamie receive email notifications when a lead changes stage? (Recommend: yes — reduces inbound status inquiries)
  • [?] Can Jamie attach a brief or intro email when submitting a lead?
  • [A] Commission rate (2%) and protection window (90 days) are assumptions — confirm with Equinix program team
  • [?] Are referral partners able to upgrade to reseller status through the portal, or does that require a new application?
  • Stub screens: ref-submit.html (dedicated lead submission), ref-commissions.html (full commission history + payment details)
