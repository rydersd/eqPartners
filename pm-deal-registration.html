Validation Rules & Edge Cases
  • All required fields (marked *) must be completed before submission
  • Deal name must be unique per customer per quarter to avoid duplicates
  • Revenue amount must be between $50K and $5M
  • Close date must be within 180 days of submission
  • Status automatically changes to "Pending" on submission, then "Approved" by PSM within 48 hours
  • Draft deals auto-delete after 30 days of inactivity
  • Protection period starts on approval date, lasts 90 days
  • During protection period, duplicate registrations are blocked with warning message
  • Won deals (Order stage) freeze all edits, but can be viewed and linked to commission
Error States & Messages
  • "Customer not found" — Trigger account lookup; offer quick add for new customers
  • "Deal under protection period" — Show existing deal details and protection end date
  • "Incomplete required fields" — Highlight missing fields, show inline validation errors
  • "Revenue outside allowed range" — Must be $50K to $5M; show error
  • "Close date in past" — Cannot register closed deals; show error
  • "Deal registration rejected" — PSM comment provided; allow re-submit after edits
  • "Draft not found" — Draft may have been deleted due to inactivity
Accessibility Notes
  • Wizard stepper uses ARIA roles (tablist, tab, tabpanel) for screen readers
  • All form inputs have associated labels and aria-required attributes
  • Status badges include color + text + aria-label (not color alone)
  • Search results announce with aria-live regions when populated
  • Keyboard navigation: Tab moves through form fields, Enter submits forms
  • Wizard buttons disable prev/next appropriately based on current step
  • Form validation errors announced to screen readers inline
  • Review step displays read-only values with clear field labels
User Flow (Numbered Steps)
  1. PM views deal registration page, sees KPI cards (active deals, pending approval, etc.)
  2. PM reviews draft deals and continues or creates new deal
  3. PM searches for existing deals to check for duplicates and protection periods
  4. PM reviews registered deals table, filters by status (all/pending/approved/won)
  5. PM clicks "Search Before You Register" to check for existing opportunities
  6. PM proceeds to wizard: Step 1 enters customer information (name, contact, industry, size)
  7. PM proceeds to Step 2: enters deal details (name, revenue, close date, products, source)
  8. PM proceeds to Step 3: identifies competitor and Equinix differentiators
  9. PM proceeds to Step 4: reviews all information, confirms terms, submits for approval
  10. PM sees success message and deal transitions to "Pending Approval" status
  11. PM can view linked Salesforce Opportunity after PSM approval
  12. Deal gets 90-day protection period during which PM can track progress
Related Automations & Flows
Salesforce Flows:
• Deal_Registration_Submission_Flow — triggered on Deal_Registration__c create, validates customer, checks for duplicates, sends PSM notification, creates Opportunity
• Duplicate_Deal_Detection_Flow — daily check for overlapping registrations within 90-day protection periods
• Deal_Status_Sync_Flow — syncs Deal_Registration__c status with Opportunity stage

Apex Triggers:
• DealRegistrationTrigger — on insert/update, enforce validation rules, update protection dates
• OpportunitySyncTrigger — on stage change, update Deal_Registration__c status

Batch & Scheduled Jobs:
• Draft_Deal_Cleanup_Batch — runs daily, deletes drafts older than 30 days
• Protection_Period_Expiry_Batch — runs daily, marks expired deals as "Closed", notifies PMs
Design Brief & Rationale
Why This Layout?
The Deal Registration page combines three critical workflows: (1) viewing and managing existing deals, (2) searching to avoid duplicates, and (3) registering new deals via a guided wizard. The KPI cards at the top give PMs immediate insight into their performance and deal pipeline. The draft deals section enables PMs to pause and resume work without losing progress. The search functionality prevents duplicate registrations and helps PMs understand market saturation.

Wizard vs. Single Form:
A 4-step wizard reduces cognitive load by breaking deal registration into logical sections (customer → deal details → competitive → review). This mirrors the sales process and ensures all critical information is captured without overwhelming PMs with a 20+ field form.

Status Badges & Visual Hierarchy:
Status badges use color + uppercase text + subtle backgrounds. The protection period banner uses green to signal positive deal conditions. Pending approval deals use orange to signal action needed.

Accessibility:
All form fields have labels, required indicators, and keyboard navigation. The wizard stepper uses ARIA roles so screen readers announce step progress. Validation errors appear inline and are announced to assistive technologies.

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • KPI cards: Active Deals, Pending Approval, Deals Protected, Won
  • Multi-step wizard form (Customer Info, Deal Details, Competitive, Review)
  • Search Before You Register tool to check duplicates and active protection periods
  • Deal registration table with sortable columns and status filters
  • Registered deals summary by status (Draft, Pending, Approved, Won)

Interactive Elements

  • Wizard stepper showing current step and validation state
  • Filter registered deals by status, rep, date range
  • Click deal to view full registration detail and protection status
  • Sort by any column header (Deal Name, Rep, Amount, Status, Close Date)
  • Export registration data and templates
  • Save draft and continue later

Functionality

  • Validation & Duplicate Prevention
    • Enforce revenue range validation ($50K–$5M) and close-date constraints (within 180 days)
    • Enforce duplicate-check and protection-period validation before approval to prevent conflicts
    • Unique deal name per customer per quarter validation to avoid duplicate registrations
  • Routing & Automation
    • Route registrations to appropriate approver based on deal size and region
    • Auto-transition status: Draft → Pending on submit, Pending → Approved by PSM within 48hr SLA
    • Auto-delete inactive drafts after 30 days to maintain system hygiene
  • Protection & Lifecycle Management
    • Auto-expire deal protection at 90-day mark with notification to registered rep
    • Lock won deals (Order stage) to read-only and block all edits to prevent unauthorized changes
