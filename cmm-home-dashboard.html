Design Notes — cmm-home-dashboard.html

Purpose

Priya Nair is Equinix's Channel Marketing Manager. She is the person nobody in the current wireframe set represented — the person who decides how much MDF Gold partners get per quarter, what activities are eligible, and what campaigns Equinix runs that partners can co-invest in. Without Priya, the MDF budget allocation process has no owner in the wireframes.

New Persona: Priya Nair (Channel Marketing Manager, Equinix)

Based on the publicly listed Equinix Partner Marketing Manager role (careers.equinix.com, Feb 2026). Responsibilities include:

  • Setting and managing MDF budgets by tier and region
  • Designing co-marketing programs partners can opt into (webinars, trade shows, content templates)
  • Monitoring partner MDF utilization and driving engagement for underutilizing partners
  • Flagging program-level policy exceptions (over-cap requests, ineligible activities) for escalation
  • Reporting program-level marketing ROI to leadership

How Priya Differs from the PAM and PSM

PAM (Alex): Approves individual partner MDF requests. Sees one partner at a time.

PSM (Marcus): Tracks partner health and enablement. MDF analytics are a secondary view.

CMM (Priya): Sets the rules the PAM enforces. Sees the full program ($12.4M budget, 51 partners, 18 campaigns) and flags structural problems: Bronze partners not engaging, EMEA under-utilizing, ROI trending down on enablement activities.

Design Decisions

Bronze utilization callout (9%)

Bronze partners are chronically low MDF utilizers across most programs. Priya's job is to notice this and act — either by simplifying the submission flow, running an outreach campaign, or recommending that Bronze partners be moved to a no-MDF tier. Surfacing the 9% prominently is intentional.

MDF requests flagged for program attention

Some MDF requests require more than PAM-level approval — they need a program policy decision (waiver, eligibility ruling). Priya's queue surfaces these before the PAM's queue so she can pre-authorize or reject at the policy level first.

Stub screens

cmm-budget-allocation.html and cmm-campaign-calendar.html and cmm-mdf-oversight.html are nav-linked but not yet built. This screen establishes the surface; those are natural follow-ons.
