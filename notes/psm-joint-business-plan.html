Design Specs

Summary

Dashboard for PSM to oversee partner joint business plans, monitor revenue targets and initiative milestones, and approve annual plan submissions with QBR scheduling.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Oversee partner joint business plans
  • Review and approve annual JBP initiatives and revenue targets
  • Monitor JBP milestone achievement and partner performance
  • Align partner plans with internal business objectives
  • Track JBP-influenced revenue opportunities
  • Support executive business reviews (QBR) with plan metrics

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • JBP dashboard with plan status overview
  • Partner list with plan completion status
  • Revenue target tracking vs. actual
  • Initiative list with owner, target date, status
  • Upcoming QBR schedule
  • Plan approval workflow queue

Interactive Elements

  • Click partner to view or edit their JBP
  • Approve/reject plan submissions with feedback
  • Update initiative status and completion
  • View revenue target progress chart
  • Schedule and manage QBR meetings
  • Export plan summaries for executive review

Functionality

  • JBP Data Retrieval & Display
    • Query all Joint_Business_Plan__c records with related Partner Account lookup and fiscal year filter
    • Fetch JBP_Initiative__c child records sorted by status and target date
    • Display plan metadata including owner, last updated date, and current approval status
  • Revenue Tracking & Progress Analytics
    • Calculate YTD revenue variance vs. quarterly targets by comparing JBP_Revenue_Target__c against Deal_Registration__c actuals
    • Aggregate deal revenue by quarter and compute % attainment against revenue goal target
    • Display forecast EOY revenue using linear projection or deal pipeline velocity model
  • Approval Workflow & Status Management
    • Route plan submissions to appropriate approver based on plan revenue amount (VP if >$3M, PSM if <$3M)
    • Trigger approval workflow rules and update JBP_Status__c field to Submitted, Approved, or Rejected upon PSM decision
    • Log approval action with timestamp and approver name for audit trail
  • Initiative & Goal Tracking
    • Display and update JBP_Initiative__c progress percentages as tasks are completed by owners
    • Calculate goal completion metrics (Revenue %, Training Certifications, Marketing Activities, Registered Deals)
    • Benchmark actual vs. target metrics and display status indicators (On Track, At Risk, Behind)
  • Reporting & Export Functionality
    • Generate exportable PDF summary with initiative progress, revenue dashboard, and goal status
    • Schedule quarterly QBR meetings by creating Event records linked to JBP and Partner Account
    • Archive completed plans and maintain historical view for trending and performance analysis

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • Joint_Business_Plan__c: Partner_Account__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Plan_Status__c, Revenue_Target__c, YTD_Revenue__c
  • JBP_Initiative__c: Plan__c, Initiative_Name__c, Owner__c, Category__c, Status__c, Target_Date__c
  • JBP_Revenue_Target__c: Plan__c, Quarter__c, Target_Amount__c, Actual_Amount__c, Variance_Pct__c

Validation & Business Logic

  • Revenue target required before plan activation (min $100K)
  • All initiatives must have owner and target date before plan submit
  • Gold partners require VP sign-off; Silver/Bronze only PSM sign-off
  • Plan auto-expires if not renewed within 30 days of fiscal year end
  • Cannot create duplicate active plan for same partner + fiscal year
