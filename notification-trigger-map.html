Notification Architecture

All notifications are dispatched via Salesforce Experience Cloud notification framework. Email templates are Marketing Cloud HTML emails with ref IDs in the PCP-xxx namespace. In-portal bell notifications use the SF Bell component. Chatter-based notifications are internal only (PSM/PAM/VP). No SMS is currently planned.

Wiring Status: Rows marked with ✓ indicate events that have corresponding wireframe screens in the portal. Rows marked with ○ Planned represent notifications that are in the roadmap but do not yet have dedicated UI screens in the wireframes.
