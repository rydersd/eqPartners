Design Notes — PSM Region View

Persona: Diana Flores, PSM Manager — oversees 4 ICs in AMER West. Cares about portfolio health distribution, QBR coverage gaps, unassigned partners, and PSM performance comparisons.

PSM table: One row per IC. Health pill reflects average across their portfolio. At-risk row (Priya Patel) highlighted in red — triggers when avg health <60 or overdue QBRs ≥2. "View →" links to that IC's psm-my-partners.html.

QBR coverage: % of partners with QBR scheduled in current or next quarter. 100% = full coverage. Below 75% triggers amber indicator.

Unassigned partners: Surface immediately at top. Triggered by: partner completes onboarding with no PSM auto-assigned (region or tier mismatch), or PSM departure leaves orphaned partners. "Assign PSM" opens a Salesforce Lightning assignment flow.

Health by tier chart: G/A/R counts per tier. Helps manager spot systemic issues (e.g., Select tier skewing red) vs. isolated PSM gaps.

Full partner list: Paginated, 10 rows default. Filters: At risk, No PSM, by tier. Table is cross-PSM — useful for reassignment conversations and QBRs.
