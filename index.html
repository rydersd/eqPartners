Partner vs. EUC data access: EUC customers see full operational detail for their own services (configs, case contents, billing, utilization). Partner account managers see only aggregated health metrics and deal status per customer account — detailed service data remains private to the EUC and Equinix account team.
Partner solution model: Partners (VAR/MSP/SI) assemble multi-vendor packages where Equinix infrastructure (Fabric, Colo, Network Edge, Smart Hands) is one layer alongside third-party technologies (Palo Alto, Cisco, Dell, Veeam, Cloudflare, Zscaler, AWS, Azure). Equinix solutions fit into the partner's overarching customer problem-solve — they are not the same as partner solutions.
MRR-Only Commissions: All partner commissions apply to MRR only. NRR (install fees, smart hands, buildouts) excluded. Rates: Bronze 8%, Silver 12%, Gold 15%. Co-sell +2%.
Phase 2 — AI Spend Optimization: Einstein-powered recommendations for ECP customers based on utilization telemetry. Requires 6+ months DCIM/SmartView data. Ships separately from PCP launch.

Cross-Platform Features

Notifications — Cross-portal alerts: approvals, tier changes, cert expiry, payout status, deal status
Deal Registration — Reps submit → PMs oversee → PSMs approve (visible across all portals)
Training Sync — SumTotal LMS → Salesforce nightly batch → visible to PSM, PM, and Rep
Tier Advancement — Calculated hourly, visible to all personas with role-appropriate detail
Near-Win Signals — Visible to PSM (spotlight + row highlight) and PM (alert banner)
Commission Tracking — Auto-calculated from orders, visible to Rep (personal) and PM (org total)
MDF Lifecycle — PM requests → PSM approves → PM executes → PM submits PoP → PSM reimburses
JBP & QBRs — Joint between PSM and PM, not visible to individual Reps
