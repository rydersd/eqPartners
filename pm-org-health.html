Design Specs

Summary

The Organization Health Dashboard provides partners with a scorecard view of their tier status, training completion, revenue progress, and pipeline health with actionable insights for tier advancement.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Monitor organization-wide health against program requirements and tier thresholds
  • Understand current tier status and progress toward next tier (Gold or higher)
  • See weighted scoring factors: training completion, revenue, deal registration, certifications
  • Track team training completion rates and identify top performers for recognition
  • View pipeline health and deal progression across all stages
  • Communicate tier advancement goals and required actions to team members

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Near-Win Alert banner flagging opportunities for immediate tier advancement
  • Team Completion card with training completion gauge and progress bar
  • Current Tier Status card showing weighted score breakdown and factor contributions
  • KPI cards: YTD Revenue, Quota Attainment %, Active Deals, Certifications Completed
  • Deal Pipeline Overview grid showing stage distribution and deal counts
  • Training Leaderboard table with member names, completion %, rank badges
  • Next Steps section with recommended actions for tier advancement

Interactive Elements

  • Progress bar visualization for team training completion with percentage
  • Tier breakdown expandable section showing weighted factor calculations
  • Tier badge indicating current status (Silver/Gold) with next tier preview
  • Pipeline stage counters displaying deal progression with stage labels
  • Leaderboard rank badges (Gold/Silver/Bronze stars) for top performers
  • Completion fill bars for each team member with color coding
  • Send Reminder and Export Report button actions for team communications

Functionality

  • Tier Scoring & Eligibility
    • Weighted tier score calculation: training_pct (30%) + revenue_ytd (35%) + deals_registered (20%) + certifications (15%) for fair assessment
    • Tier eligibility threshold comparison: current_score vs. tier_minimums with visual gap analysis to show advancement path
  • Data Aggregation & Refresh
    • Training completion aggregation from LMS/SumTotal with daily refresh for current metrics
    • Revenue YTD sum from closed-won opportunities with quota comparison and attainment percentage
  • Alerts, Ranking & Reporting
    • Near-win alert trigger: If (current_score >= 95% of next_tier_threshold), show banner to prompt action
    • Leaderboard auto-ranking based on training completion with tie-breaking by recent activity for transparency
    • Export report function: PDF generation of dashboard snapshot with team metrics and recommendations for stakeholder sharing
