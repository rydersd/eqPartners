System Specification
Data Objects & Aggregation
Primary Objects
  • Account: Customer organization entity with partner assignment and MRR baseline
  • Customer_Health_Score__c: Calculated health metric updated daily with weighted component scores
  • ServiceContract: Service line items linked to Account; displayed as count only
  • Case: Support tickets aggregated by status and aging; partner view shows count only, not details
  • Asset: Infrastructure resource count per customer; available as inventory total
Data Access & Validation Rules
Partner Portal Permissions
  • Partners see AGGREGATE metrics only: health score, case count, MRR, service count, renewal dates
  • Partners do NOT see: detailed case content, service configurations, billing line items, customer usage logs
  • Partners see contact names/roles but NOT email addresses except for assigned Account Executives
  • Revenue data limited to MRR overview; detailed billing and pricing are not visible
  • Boundary enforcement: Data masking applied at API and database query level
Interaction Patterns
  • Health score updates trigger when component metrics change (daily calculation window)
  • Expansion triggers: New service deployments, MRR growth, reduced case load
  • Churn risk triggers: Health score drops, overdue renewals, contact role changes
  • Portal refresh: Health dashboard updates hourly; renewal calendar displays 6-month forward window
Design Intent & Bridge Function

This Customer Health Overview page serves as a bridge between Partner Portal and Customer Portal. It gives partners enough visibility to manage customer relationships effectively—renewal planning, health trends, engagement signals—while maintaining information security boundaries. Partners can:

  • Identify at-risk accounts and take proactive renewal action
  • Understand service expansion opportunities based on health trends
  • Coordinate with Equinix Account Executives on joint customer success activities
  • Access aggregated metrics without exposing operational, billing, or sensitive support details

The boundary between partner view (this page) and operational view (internal systems) is enforced at the data layer to ensure partners never encounter sensitive information even during edge cases or system changes.

Design Specs

Summary

The Customer Health Dashboard provides partners with a unified view of account health across their customer portfolio, enabling proactive renewal planning and risk identification while maintaining strict data security boundaries.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Monitor aggregate health metrics across entire customer portfolio
  • Identify at-risk accounts requiring renewal action
  • Track customer engagement through health score trends
  • Plan renewal conversations with visibility into key contact info
  • Understand health methodology and scoring factors
  • Maintain data security boundaries (aggregate metrics, no case details)

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • KPI cards: Active Customers, Total MRR, Health distribution
  • Priority Alerts section for urgent renewal dates and at-risk customers
  • Customer Health Dashboard table with sortable columns
  • Expandable customer detail section with health score, revenue, services
  • Renewal Calendar grid (next 6 months)
  • Health Score Methodology showing 5 weighted calculation factors

Interactive Elements

  • Expandable/collapsible customer detail sections
  • Health score badges (Excellent/Good/Fair/Poor color coding)
  • Risk level badges showing Low/Medium/High status
  • Renewal alerts highlighted in amber for urgent dates
  • Revenue trend chart with 6 monthly bars for selected customer
  • Data boundary notice explaining partner data visibility restrictions

Functionality

  • Data Integration & Aggregation
    • Salesforce Account/ServiceContract queries for customer list and MRR baseline
    • Daily health score calculation aggregating uptime, support cases, revenue, engagement, and relationship factors
  • Access Control & Data Security
    • Role-based data masking ensures partners see aggregate metrics only—health scores, case counts, MRR, service counts, renewal dates
    • Boundary enforcement applied at API and database query level to prevent access to case content, billing details, and customer usage logs
  • Alerting & Risk Scoring
    • Renewal date alerting triggered within 90-day forward window with visual highlighting
    • Risk scoring rule engine flagging health drops, overdue renewals, and contact role changes for priority action
