Design Specs

Summary

First-login account setup for invited users — reps and secondary admins who join a partner org via invitation. Four-step flow: Welcome → Security Setup (MFA) → Your Profile → Ready to Go. MFA is required at this step because invited users haven't gone through the main onboarding wizard. Team setup and admin orientation are handled by the org admin (Jordan) in the onboarding-wizard.html flow — not here.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Orient a new partner admin to their admin role and responsibilities
  • Enable immediate team-building (queue rep invitations before leaving setup)
  • Explain tier mechanics so the admin understands what drives advancement
  • Surface deal registration and MDF at a conceptual level — not overwhelming, just enough to know it exists
  • Connect admin to their PSM and support resources

Design Specification

Step 4 — Ready to Go

  • Confirmation state showing setup complete for invited users
  • On "Go to Dashboard" click: User.IsFirstLogin__c → false; redirect to pm-home-day0.html
  • Links to Training (first task most invited reps need) as a secondary CTA
  • Team setup / admin orientation removed — that's the org admin's job in onboarding-wizard.html

Navigation

  • Nav links greyed and aria-disabled until onboarding completes
  • Active step pulsing ring on step-number circle
  • Completed steps show ✓ check (font-size:0 + ::after content trick)

Persona & Routing

  • Persona badge: persona-badge--pm (Partner Manager Admin)
  • Portal: Experience Cloud — PM Portal
  • On wizard completion: User.IsFirstLogin__c=false → redirect to pm-home-dashboard.html
  • Queued invitations trigger bulk Contact+User provisioning flow on completion

Salesforce Objects

  • User: IsFirstLogin__c, MFA_Verified__c, Portal_Role__c = 'Admin'
  • Onboarding_Step__c: Step_Order__c, Completed__c, Skipped__c (same as rep)
  • Pending_Invitation__c: Inviter_User__c, Email__c, Role__c, Status__c, Sent_Date__c
  • Partner_Account__c: Tier__c, MDF_Balance__c, Revenue_YTD__c
  • Provisioning flow: on wizard complete, batch-process Pending_Invitation__c WHERE Status='Queued'

Accessibility Notes

  • Tier table uses scope="col" on headers and role="table" + aria-label
  • Invite tag × controls have aria-label="Remove" and role="button" + keyboard handler
  • Greyed nav links carry aria-disabled="true" and tabindex="-1"
  • Progress bar: role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max
  • Active step has aria-current="step"
