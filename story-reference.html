Story Reference

The shared fiction that anchors all wireframes in this project. Use this page to align on who the characters are, what companies they work for, and what motivates them — so every screen tells a consistent story.

⚠ Proto-personas — validation required. Steve, Jordan, and Marcus are design-fiction anchors used to keep wireframe flows consistent. They draw on public job posting data, BLS stats, and industry surveys, but none have been validated through user interviews, field research, or a formal recruitment and moderated study. Do not treat them as research-backed personas until that work is done. They should be revisited before any feature prioritization decisions are made against them.

About This Page

Design reference — not a deliverable. This page is internal to the wireframe project and should appear at the very bottom of the sitemap, outside the normal portal navigation tree.

Intended audience

Wireframe designers, stakeholders reviewing the prototype, anyone who needs to check "what's Steve's email?" without hunting through each wireframe file.

What to do with this in production

This page has no equivalent in the real portal. It exists only to anchor the design fiction. Remove from any deliverable that goes to development.

On the "Portal Admin" title

Jordan Reeves's actual title should be "Sales Operations Manager" or "Partner Operations Manager" in any realistic company of CloudBridge's size. "Portal Admin" is used as a role label within the portal, not a job title.

Why two CloudBridge entities?

Region-scoped company search in the registration wizard needs a second entity to demonstrate what a "region mismatch" looks like. CloudBridge EMEA Ltd. serves that purpose without inventing an unrelated company.
