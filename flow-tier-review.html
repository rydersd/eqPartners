Tier Review Cycle Process Flow

Portal: Equinix Partner Connect (PCP) Version: 1.0 Updated: 2026-02-27

Key Business Rules

  • Reviews triggered annually (Q4) and on significant score change (±15 points)
  • Tier thresholds: Platinum ≥95, Gold ≥75, Silver ≥50, Bronze <50
  • Platinum partners: PSM review required even for Maintain decisions
  • Upgrade requires 2 consecutive periods at qualifying score
  • Downgrade: 1st period = warning, 2nd consecutive missed = action
  • Remediation period: 90 days, one per 24-month period; score recalculated mid-period
  • Appeals: 30-day window from demotion notice; one appeal per review cycle
  • Tier changes take effect on next agreement renewal date (not immediately)

Outcome Paths Summary

Upgrade PAM authorizes → Amendment flow → Partner notified → Benefits activate on renewal
Maintain Auto-approved by PAM → No action → Optional partner notification → Status quo continues
Downgrade PAM confirms demotion → Partner notified → 30-day appeal window → Execute on renewal
Remediation PAM grants 90-day remediation period → Partner works on improvement plan → Mid-period re-evaluation scheduled

SLAs & Timelines

  • PAM Review: 5 business days
  • Appeal Window: 30 days from demotion notice
  • Remediation Period: 90 days (one per 24-month period)
  • Tier Change Effective: Next agreement renewal date
  • Annual Review: Q4 (October–December)

Tier Thresholds

Platinum ≥95 points
Gold ≥75 points
Silver ≥50 points
Bronze <50 points

Key Actors

  • Marcus Chen: PSM at Equinix (reviewer)
  • Alex Rivera: PAM at Equinix (authorizer)
  • Jordan Reeves: Partner Manager, CloudBridge (recipient)

Scoring Formula

  • Revenue YTD (40%)
  • Active Certifications (20%)
  • Deal Registrations Filed (15%)
  • Win Rate (15%)
  • Program Tenure (10%)
