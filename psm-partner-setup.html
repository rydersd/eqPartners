Purpose

After application approval, PSM configures the new partner organization record before provisioning portal access. This page controls tier assignment, territory, product tracks, and triggers automated account creation across Salesforce, Experience Cloud, and SumTotal.

Functionality

Tier Assignment
  • New partners always begin at Registered tier regardless of existing relationships. Tier advancement must be earned through MRR, certification, and deal activity thresholds. PSM cannot manually override the starting tier.
  • Commission rate and MDF eligibility are automatically set based on tier selection and cannot be manually overridden on this screen.
Product Track Configuration
  • PSM selects which Equinix product lines the partner is authorized to sell. Selection drives: certification requirements in SumTotal, commission schedule mapping, deal registration product eligibility, and co-marketing fund eligibility.
  • Tracks can be added later via the partner's Account record but cannot be removed without PSM Manager approval.
Auto-Provisioning on Complete Setup
  • Triggers simultaneous creation of: Salesforce Account record, two Experience Cloud user accounts (PM + Technical Contact), SumTotal training enrollment, welcome email, and a kickoff call task assigned to the PSM.
  • The partner's PM will receive a portal invite at their registered email within 10 minutes of provisioning completion.
Training Timeline
  • Cert due dates are auto-calculated from the account activation date. The system creates training reminders in SumTotal and surfaces alerts in the PSM's dashboard if certs fall overdue.

Key Settings

  • Starting tier (always Registered) · Territory assignment · PSM ownership · Product track eligibility · Provisioning checklist
