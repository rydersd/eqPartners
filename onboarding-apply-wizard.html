Design Notes

Summary

Partner Organization Registration wizard — Variant A (search-before-create). 4-step wizard: Company & You → Industry → Business → Review. Company search triggers a modal showing matching Salesforce Account records; selecting one pre-fills the company fields. Step 3 content renders conditionally based on partner type chosen in Step 1. Step 4 summarizes all inputs before submission. See onboarding-apply-form.html for Variant B (no search, manual entry throughout).

Figma Source

  • File: Partner-Central
  • Node: 2311-32822
  • Variants covered: Reseller form + Lead Referral form (both handled via conditional Step 3)

Flow Position

  • Flow: Partner Organization Registration & Onboarding — Step 1 (Apply)
  • Entry points: Equinix.com partner landing → "Apply"; PSM invite link; Partner Central signup CTA
  • Pre-auth: Yes — public page, no login required
  • On submit: → onboarding-agreement-confirmation.html (email confirmation + MSA sent)

Jobs to Be Done

  • Reduce abandonment by splitting a long form into 3 logical chunks with clear progress
  • Prevent duplicate Salesforce Account records via search-before-create in Step 1
  • Show users exactly what they're selecting with the partner type cards before asking for details
  • Collect enough data for the PSM to qualify and configure the account without a follow-up call

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • 3-step stepper: Company & You · Industry · Business
  • Step 1: partner type radio cards + company search → modal → pre-fill + contact info (merged)
  • Step 2: vertical markets (8 → checkbox grid), regions (6 → checkbox grid), customer size (3 → checkbox grid)
  • Step 3 Reseller: employee count (5, radio grid), revenue (5, radio grid), business mix %, cloud solutions (7, checkbox grid), cloud providers (8, checkbox grid)
  • Step 3 Referral: business type (dropdown, 8 options → select), lead gen method (5, checkbox grid), deal value (4, radio grid)

Interactive Elements

  • Partner type cards — radio group; drives Step 3 conditional render
  • Company search → openSearchModal() shows overlay modal with 2 matching records
  • Modal result click → selectCompanyResult(id) pre-fills form fields, closes modal, shows selected company banner
  • "My company isn't listed" → clears search, shows manual entry form
  • Selected company banner — "Change" button resets back to search state
  • Checkbox grids + radio grids — whole tile clickable, highlights on check
  • Business mix: live running total with colour feedback (blue = 100%, red ≠ 100%)

Functionality

  • openSearchModal() — reads input, sets modal subhead with query text, opens overlay
  • selectCompanyResult(id) — 'cloudbridge-us': fills name=CloudBridge Technologies Inc., website=cloudbridge.com, country=United States, city=Austin, street=500 West 2nd Street Suite 1900; 'cloudbridge-emea': fills London variant; 'new': shows blank manual form
  • Pre-fill pattern — filled inputs get .prefilled class (tinted background) so user sees what was populated vs. blank
  • goToStep(n) — hides all steps, activates target, updates stepper; triggers renderStep3() if n=3
  • UX rule applied: options < 10 → checkbox/radio grid; options ≥ 10 → <select> (Language, Country, Business Type)

Design Notes

  • Modal "click outside to close" is implemented — escape key should also close (production)
  • Pre-filled fields remain editable — user may need to correct stale Salesforce data
  • Salesforce duplicate detection should run on company name + domain before showing results in production
  • Partner type can't be changed after Step 1 without going back — consider a visible type indicator in steps 2 and 3 so user remembers what they selected
  • Companion file onboarding-apply-form.html = Variant B (no search), same 3-step structure
