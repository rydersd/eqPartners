How Deal Registration Works

Follow these five steps to register deals and earn commissions with Equinix.

Search for Existing Deals
Before creating a new registration, search our database to ensure you're not registering a duplicate. This protects both you and Equinix from conflicts.
Complete Registration Form
Fill in customer details, product/service, estimated deal value, and close date. Our system calculates your potential commission in real time.
Submit for Review
Submit your registration. Our Partnerships team reviews it within 2 business days and approves qualified deals that meet our criteria.
Track & Update
Once approved, track your deal's progress through pipeline stages. Update the deal status as it moves closer to close.
Earn Commission
When your deal closes, commission is calculated and paid on the 15th of the following month. Check your commission summary to see earnings.

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Commission summary heading "Commission Summary (Year-to-Date)"
  • Active deals table with columns: Deal Name, Account, Amount, Status (Registered/Pending/Expired), Commission Rate, Est. Commission
  • Estimated Commission calculator box showing Commission Rate (Silver tier example: 10%) and calculated Est. Commission
  • Deal Registration section with dropdowns for Product and input for Deal Value
  • Commission Statement section with deal registration process steps (visual numbered steps 1-5)
  • Key information boxes explaining commission policies and tier rates
  • Sidebar with Deal Registration instructions and quick reference tables

Interactive Elements

  • Product dropdown in commission calculator
  • Deal Value input field for real-time commission estimate
  • Active deals table sortable by columns
  • Deal rows clickable (navigate to opportunity detail)
  • Filter/search functionality for deals
  • Download commission statement link

Functionality

  • Commission Calculation & Summary
    • Commission summary calculated from Opportunity records: SUM(Opportunity.Amount WHERE Status='Closed Won') * Commission_Rate__c looked up from Account.Partner_Tier__c
    • Commission Rate fetched from Partner_Tier_Rates__mdt custom metadata type; rates vary by Partner_Tier__c (Bronze, Silver, Gold) and Product_Category__c (e.g., 5% Bronze Colocation, 10% Silver Interconnection)
    • Summary dashboard displays YTD commission, quarterly breakdown, and tier benefits at top of page
  • Deal Registration & Active Deal Tracking
    • Active Deals table queries Partner_Deal_Registration__c WHERE Status__c='Registered' with linked Opportunity details (Name, Amount, Product, Stage, Registration_Date__c)
    • Deal Status displayed from Partner_Deal_Registration__c.Status__c enum (Registered, Pending, Expired); visually styled with badges
    • Deals sortable by column (Deal Name, Amount, Status, Registration Date); table re-renders on sort change
  • Commission Estimation & Calculator
    • Commission Calculator updates Estimated Commission field in real-time: Estimated_Commission = Deal_Value_Input * (Product.Commission_Rate__c from Partner_Tier_Rates__mdt filtered by logged-in user's Account.Partner_Tier__c)
    • Product dropdown populated from Product__c records; onChange event triggers commission recalculation
    • Deal Value input field fires onchange event to recalculate commission dynamically; displays updated estimate without page reload
  • Commission Tracking & Reporting
    • Commission Tracking queryable by quarter (YTD, Q1-Q4), deal status (Registered, Pending, Expired), and individual deal registration
    • Commission_Tracking__c custom object stores historical records: Opportunity__c lookup, Commission_Amount__c, Payment_Status__c, Payment_Date__c
    • Download Commission Statement link exports commission history to PDF/CSV with quarterly totals and payment details

Technical Details

Salesforce Objects & Rates

  • Opportunity__c with Amount, StageName, Partner_Deal_Registration__c lookup
  • Partner_Deal_Registration__c with Opportunity__c, Product__c, Status__c (Registered|Pending|Expired), Registration_Date__c
  • Commission_Tracking__c custom object for historical tracking: Opportunity__c, Commission_Amount__c, Payment_Status__c, Payment_Date__c
  • Partner_Tier_Rates__mdt custom metadata with Tier__c, Product_Category__c, Commission_Rate__c (e.g., 10% for Silver Colocation)
  • Account.Partner_Tier__c field determines commission rate multiplier
  • Commission calculated via Salesforce formula field or scheduled flow: Amount * (Product.Commission_Rate__c from CMT)
