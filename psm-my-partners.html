Summary

Salesforce Lightning list view for the PSM IC (Marcus Chen). Shows all 8 assigned partners with health score, pipeline, QBR status, and pending actions — including a queue of partner-submitted directory changes awaiting PSM approval before publishing to equinix.com.

Directory Review Queue

  • Partners can submit changes to solution areas and geographic reach via pm-directory-listing.html
  • These fields are PSM-gated — they appear in a review queue here before publishing
  • PSM approves or rejects each change · rejection triggers an email to partner with reason
  • SLA: 2–3 business days per Equinix directory policy
  • Approved changes sync to equinix.com partner-finder.html within 24h (batch sync)

Health Score Logic

  • Green: ≥70 · Amber: 50–69 · Red: <50
  • Calculated from: Training completion (30%) + Revenue vs target (40%) + Active deals (30%)
  • Updated hourly from Salesforce batch job
  • At-risk indicator: health <50 OR QBR overdue >30 days OR no active deals >90 days
