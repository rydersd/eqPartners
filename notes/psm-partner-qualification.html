Design Specs

Summary

Evaluates prospective partner applications against 8 core qualification criteria including financial health, technical capability, and territory alignment. PSMs use this assessment form to approve or defer partner qualification with documented rationale.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Systematically evaluate prospective partner quality, financial health, and strategic fit
  • Apply consistent 8-criterion qualification standard across all applicants
  • Document technical capability and territory risks for audit trail
  • Support approve/defer/reject decisions with clear rationale
  • Route qualified partners to formal onboarding process

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Prospect header: Company name, primary contact, industry, country, submission date
  • Qualification Criteria checklist (8 items): Annual Revenue, Technical Expertise, Financial Health, Customer References, Insurance/Liability, Legal Compliance, Territory Fit, Partnership Type Alignment
  • Territory Overlap card with flag alerts if existing partner overlap detected
  • Financial Health card showing overall score (0-100), assessment components (cash flow, debt ratio, revenue growth, years in business)
  • Technical Capability assessment grid with star ratings for Cloud Expertise, Infrastructure, Network Design, Implementation
  • Recommendation panel with Go/No-Go badge and supporting rationale text
  • Activity & Insights right rail with recent application milestones and Agentforce AI recommendations

Interactive Elements

  • Approve Partner, Defer Decision, Reject buttons in recommendation panel
  • Activity/Agentforce tab toggle in right rail
  • Territory overlap expandable details to review coordination status
  • Contact name clickable to view applicant details
  • Request Info and Reject buttons in actions panel

Functionality

  • Application Data Loading
    • Loads Partner_Application__c record including prospect company details (industry, country, revenue), submission date, and linked assessment records
    • Fetches related child records: Technical_Assessment__c, Reference_Check__c, Insurance_Verification__c, Territory_Overlap_Check__c for qualification evaluation
  • Qualification Checklist Evaluation
    • Auto-calculates 8-item qualification checklist from sub-records: Annual Revenue ($28.5M vs $10M+ threshold), Technical Expertise (star ratings), Financial Health (D&B score), Customer References count, Insurance/Liability verification, Legal Compliance checks, Territory Fit analysis, Partnership Type alignment
    • Formula evaluates: criteria_passed_count >= 8 → All Passed; 6-7 → Conditional; < 6 → Not Recommended badge display
  • Financial Health Assessment
    • Pulls credit score (0-100) from integrated Dun & Bradstreet service or cached assessment; calculates composite from Cash Flow ratio, Debt-to-Revenue ratio (0.32), Annual Revenue growth rate, Years in Business
    • Displays progress bar and color-coded score (green 75+, yellow 50-74, red <50)
  • Territory & Competitive Overlap Detection
    • Queries existing Account/Partner records with Billing_Country__c matching prospect region; calculates overlap percentage by comparing service territory match
    • Raises yellow warning flag if overlap > 25%; displays "Resolution in Progress" status with coordination contact
  • AI-Powered Recommendation & Approval Actions
    • Agentforce NBA analyzes Financial Health score + Technical Capability ratings to return approval confidence percentage and strategic fit recommendation
    • Approve button transitions Partner_Application__c.Status__c to 'Approved' and triggers Partner welcome email flow to applicant contact
    • Defer button preserves all assessment data and creates Task with Due Date = TODAY() + 30 days for PSM follow-up
    • Reject button logs rejection_reason__c and sends template rejection email via email action to applicant
