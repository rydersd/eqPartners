Design Notes

Summary

The Course Catalog enables Partner Managers to discover, review, and assign training courses to their teams based on track (Business/Technical), level, and compliance requirements.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Browse available training courses for team assignment
  • Identify courses needed for tier advancement
  • Assign courses to individual reps or entire team
  • Track course popularity and adoption
  • Support training planning and resource allocation

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Course catalog browse organized by Business and Technical tracks
  • Course cards with title, track/level badges, category, duration, format
  • Enrollment status (Enrolled vs. Not Enrolled) indicators
  • Search bar with keyword matching
  • Filters: Track, Category, Level, Format
  • Course completion stats across organization

Interactive Elements

  • Search courses by keyword and full-text matching
  • Filter by category, difficulty level, track, format
  • Click course card to view detail and enrollment status
  • Enroll/Continue buttons contextual to enrollment status
  • Sort by popularity, newest, relevance
  • View organization completion rate per course

Functionality

  • Data Integration
    • LMS/SumTotal API integration for real-time course enrollment status and course catalog sync
    • Tier-requirement rule engine to flag recommended courses per role and certification level
  • Search & Discovery
    • Full-text search with faceted filtering by track, category, level, and format
    • Keyword matching across course titles, descriptions, and instructor names
  • Automation & Assignment
    • Bulk assignment API to enroll multiple team members in selected courses
    • Automatic enrollment status tracking (Enrolled vs. Not Enrolled indicators)
  • Analytics & Reporting
    • Analytics aggregation for org-wide completion tracking and progress monitoring
    • Course popularity and adoption metrics with team-level insights
