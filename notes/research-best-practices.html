Research Document: This page summarizes industry best practices gleaned from analyzing Snowflake's Partner Network (SPN) and other leading partner program portals. Findings inform the design and feature roadmap for the Equinix Partner Channel Program (PCP).

Snowflake Partner Network (SPN) — What They Do Well

Snowflake's partner portal serves as a comprehensive model for best-in-class partner engagement. Key strengths include:

Central Portal Experience

SPN functions as a one-stop-shop for partners, consolidating:

  • Deal registration and opportunity tracking
  • Training and enablement content
  • Sales and marketing tools and collateral
  • Commission and incentive information
  • Partner resources and documentation

Data-Driven Approach

  • Uses proprietary AI/ML algorithms to automatically assign quality scores to customer references and deal registrations
  • Leverages Snowflake's Secure Data Sharing for transparent partner engagement metrics
  • Partners see real-time data on deal performance and commission calculations

Clear Deal Registration Process

  • Streamlined deal registration that accelerates opportunity velocity
  • Automatic connection to field sales team for support
  • Partners gain clear visibility into deal status and progress
  • Duplicate detection prevents conflicts and confusion

Tiered Incentive Structure

Partner tiers (Select, Premier, Elite) with escalating rewards:

  • Registered Partners: 5% GACV incentives
  • Select/Elite/Premier Tiers: 10% GACV incentives plus co-sell rewards
  • Services Funds Program (SFP): 3-5% variable funding for POCs, migrations, and implementations

Co-Sell Program

Partners earn rewards for pre-sales engagements, not just closed deals:

  • Credit for customer introductions
  • Rewards for discovery workshops and assessments
  • Incentives for POC participation and support

Self-Service Access

  • Once a partner company is registered, any employee can self-register for portal access
  • Portal admin manages company-wide user access and permissions
  • Reduces friction and increases adoption across partner organizations

Deal Registration Best Practices (Industry-Wide)

Analysis of leading partner programs reveals consistent patterns in deal registration implementation:

Search Before Create: Implement duplicate detection matching on account + deal name + timeframe. Surface potential matches in real-time as partners enter key fields. This prevents duplicate registrations and reduces manual conflict resolution.
Guided Forms with Validation: Build guided deal registration with "Needs Info" status and clear checklist for fix-and-resubmit without requiring email. Partners can independently correct incomplete submissions.
Reduce Friction: If partners can't register deals quickly, they sell something else or email everything. Best practice: keep deal registration to under 5 minutes from start to finish.
Status Visibility: Provide timelines, timestamps, and notifications throughout the deal lifecycle. When partners can see deal status and progress, they're less likely to create duplicates and more likely to follow up appropriately.
CRM Integration: Partner portal forms should auto-create opportunities in the internal CRM with complete details. This enables real-time duplicate checking and prevents data silos.
Conflict Resolution: When a duplicate is found, show the existing deal's status and owner rather than simply blocking the submission. Provide clear escalation path for disputes.

Partner Type-Specific Deal Flows

Different partner business models require different deal registration approaches:

Resellers

Sell directly to end customers. Deal registration should capture complete customer details, deal size, timeline, and solution details. Standard flow applies.

Technology Solutions Distributors (TSDs)

Sell through downstream resellers. Must capture the reseller relationship, commission split, and co-sell responsibilities. Requires deal ownership clarity.

Distributors

Handle high-volume aggregate deals. Need bulk registration capabilities and volume-based incentives. May register multiple customer opportunities per submission.

Best Practice: Auto-detect partner type from account record based on company classification, but allow manual override for edge cases. Include conditional form sections that appear/disappear based on partner type selection.

Recommendations for Equinix PCP

Based on research findings, the following recommendations should guide PCP design and development:

1. Implement Search-Before-Create as Screen Flow with Apex

Build a Salesforce Screen Flow that performs real-time duplicate detection using Apex when partners enter account name, deal name, and timeframe. Surface matches immediately and allow partners to claim existing deals if appropriate.

2. Add Partner Type Field with Conditional Sections

Add partner_type__c field to Deal_Registration__c object with values: Reseller, TSD, Distributor. Use conditional visibility to show/hide form sections based on partner type selection.

3. Expand Beyond Closed Deals (Co-Sell Model)

Design incentive structure that rewards pre-sales engagement: customer introductions, discovery workshops, POCs, and migrations — not just closed deals. Model after Snowflake's approach.

4. Plan AI/ML Deal Scoring (Phase 2)

Implement Einstein Prediction Builder to automatically score deal quality based on historical conversion rates, customer profile, deal size, and timeline. Provide visibility to partners.

5. Target < 5 Minute Deal Registration

Design deal registration forms to be completable in under 5 minutes. Test with partners and remove non-essential fields. Friction drives partners to alternative channels.

6. Build "Needs Info" Status with Checklist

When deals are rejected due to missing info, set status to "Needs Info" and provide clear checklist of required fields. Allow partners to resubmit without emailing support.

7. Add Notification Preferences

Allow partners to control alert frequency and notification channels (email, SMS, portal). Prevent notification fatigue which drives portal abandonment.

UX Principles from Top Partner Portals

Consumer-grade user experience is critical to partner program success. Poor UX is cited as the #1 reason partner programs fail to achieve adoption:

Consumer-Grade Design

Clean, modern interfaces with generous whitespace. Avoid cluttered layouts and overwhelming navigation.

Multiple Navigation Paths

Place primary functions (deal registration) on homepage AND in main navigation. Don't bury critical workflows.

Mobile Responsive

Design for field partners accessing portals on mobile devices. Ensure forms and workflows work on tablets and phones.

Minimize Portal Logins

Implement SSO with company identity providers. Password resets and credential management drive churn.

Real-Time Commission Estimates

Show estimated commission and incentives during deal registration. Transparency drives engagement and accuracy.

Celebrate Wins

Highlight deal closures, tier promotions, and milestone achievements. Recognition drives repeat engagement and loyalty.
