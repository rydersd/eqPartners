Infrastructure Inventory Specifications:

Infrastructure inventory shows all S.H.I.E.L.D. assets provisioned through Stark Industries. EUC cannot provision or decommission assets directly — all changes must be requested via support case or through their Stark Industries account manager (James Rhodes). The inventory is read-only from the EUC perspective. Assets in 'Provisioning' status are tied to open order ORD-2025-0156. The monthly costs shown are Equinix wholesale rates billed to Stark Industries — S.H.I.E.L.D.'s actual invoiced amount (EQX-INV-2026-0089, $47,820/mo) reflects Stark's markup and additional managed services.
