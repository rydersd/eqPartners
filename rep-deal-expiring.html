Design Specs

Summary

Displays deal detail with urgency highlighting days until protection expiration. Sarah Connor's Global Finance Corp deal (DR-2026-4422, $95K TCV, Proposal stage) shows 60-day protection window expiring alert. Provides guided next steps, risk assessment, deal stage progress, activity log, and quick action buttons for managing time-sensitive partnership revenue deals.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Rep reviews deal health and understands exactly how much time remains before protection expires
  • Guided next steps provide a clear action plan to close the deal or extend protection
  • Risk factors highlight competitive threats and blockers requiring attention
  • One-click escalation to PSM for executive support or fast-track approvals
  • Similar wins provide social proof and benchmark timelines for closing
  • Activity log ensures full engagement history is documented for PSM review during extension requests

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Breadcrumb navigation (Deals > Pipeline > CloudCorp Multi-Region)
  • Urgency banner with countdown timer (bold amber, large type) showing "18 Days Remaining" and expiration date
  • Deal title block with MRR amount ($125,000 ARR), deal stage, expected close date
  • Deal header action buttons: Request Extension, Convert to Order, Log Activity
  • Protection Period Timeline card with visual bar showing elapsed vs. remaining time (e.g., 72% complete)
  • Deal Stage Progress with 4-step flow (Discovery → Qualification → Proposal → Close) showing current stage
  • 2-column layout: main content area + right sidebar
  • Next Steps ordered list (1-4) with titles, descriptions, and inline action links
  • Risk Factors list with severity badges (High/Medium/Low) and icons
  • Activity Log with timeline dots, dates, actor names, event descriptions
  • Customer Contacts and Equinix Team contact cards with avatars, names, titles

Interactive Elements

  • Request Extension button — opens modal form for extension justification
  • Convert to Order button — initiates opportunity close workflow
  • Log Activity button — opens activity logging form (call/email/meeting)
  • Numbered next-step action links (e.g., "Schedule Follow-up Meeting", "Open Pricing Tool")
  • Contact names/cards clickable (navigate to contact record)
  • Breadcrumb links for navigation

Functionality

  • Deal Data Loading & Display
    • Deal record (Opportunity__c) queried with metadata: Name, MRR__c, Amount, StageName, CloseDate, Protection_Start_Date__c, Protection_End_Date__c, Account__c lookup
    • Deal header displays title, opportunity owner, account name, stage badge; all populated from Opportunity.__c fields
    • Customer Contacts fetched from Account.Contacts SOQL query; Equinix Team members from Opportunity_Team__c with roles (PSM, Account Manager, etc.)
  • Urgency & Expiration Calculations
    • Countdown timer calculated as DAYS(Protection_End_Date__c, TODAY()); shows remaining days in large amber text
    • Urgency banner displayed conditionally: IF countdown <= 30 THEN show amber alert with "Deal expires in X days" message; otherwise hidden
    • Protection Timeline percentage calculated: (DAYS(Protection_Start_Date__c, TODAY()) / DAYS(Protection_Start_Date__c, Protection_End_Date__c)) × 100; visual bar fill updated accordingly
  • Deal Progress & Workflow
    • Deal Stage Progress fetches Opportunity.StageName and maps to 4-step visual flow (Prospecting → Qualification → Proposal → Closed); current stage highlighted in amber
    • Next Steps generated from Next_Step__c junction objects linked to Opportunity; ordered by Step_Order__c (max 4 displayed); each includes Title__c, Description__c, ActionLink__c
    • Stage transitions calculated by comparing current StageName to historical stage changes; completed stages marked with filled dots
  • Risk Analysis & Activity Tracking
    • Risk Factors queried from Deal_Risk__c objects WHERE Deal__c=[current opportunity]; categorized and sorted by Severity__c (High|Medium|Low)
    • Activity Log populated from ActivityHistory OR custom Event/Task records; displayed chronologically with activity type, date, and summary
    • Risk badges colored by severity: High (red), Medium (amber), Low (gray)
  • Actions & Extensions
    • Request Extension button creates DealExtension__c record with Opportunity__c lookup, Requested_Days__c, Reason__c; triggers Flow that notifies PSM via email/Slack
    • Convert to Order button initiates opportunity close workflow; redirects to Order creation form with Opportunity linked
    • Log Activity button opens modal/form to create Task/Event records related to Opportunity

Technical Details

Salesforce Objects & Integration

  • Opportunity__c object with fields: Name, MRR__c, Amount, StageName, CloseDate, Protection_Start_Date__c, Protection_End_Date__c, Account__c lookup
  • Deal_Risk__c custom object with fields: Deal__c (lookup), Risk_Type__c, Severity__c (High|Medium|Low), Description__c, DetectedDate__c
  • Next_Step__c custom object (junction) with Deal__c, Step_Order__c, Title__c, Description__c, ActionLink__c URL
  • Opportunity_Team__c for PSM and team member assignments
  • DealExtension__c custom object captures extension requests with Reason__c, Requested_Days__c, Approval_Status__c
  • Flow auto-creates Next_Step__c records on Opportunity creation; updates Status__c when actions completed
