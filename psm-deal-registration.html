Design Notes

Summary

Deal Registration Queue showing active registrations including CloudBridge Solutions Secure Connect (DR-2026-4401) with CloudBridge Solutions — $1.62M TCV, Approved status, no conflicts. Queue illustrates duplicate detection, SLA compliance, channel conflict resolution workflows for partner deal registration and PSM approval processes.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Approve or reject partner deal registrations
  • Ensure registration compliance with program rules
  • Assess deal eligibility and deal protection claims
  • Manage deal registration SLAs and timelines
  • Provide guidance to partners on registration issues
  • Track deal registration trends and partner performance

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Deal registration queue with status indicators
  • Registration detail record view
  • Partner and opportunity summary cards
  • Approval history timeline
  • Deal protection eligibility panel

Interactive Elements

  • Approve / Reject / Request Info action buttons
  • Status filter: Pending / Approved / Rejected / Expired
  • Priority flag toggle
  • Inline comments for partner communication
  • Bulk approval selection checkboxes
  • SLA countdown indicators

Functionality

  • Registration Routing & Assignment
    • Route registrations to appropriate approver based on deal size and region via workflow rule
    • Assign based on AppovalQueue__c derived from Deal_Amount__c and Region__c lookup
    • Auto-escalate if unactioned within 48-hour review window SLA
  • Duplicate Detection & Conflict Prevention
    • Enforce duplicate-check on Deal_Registration__c before approval matching end-customer + product family
    • Query existing approved registrations within 90-day lookback period for same customer/product
    • Display conflict warning with option to override if territory already protected
  • SLA Management & Auto-Expiration
    • Auto-expire registrations past SLA window (48-hour review period) with status change to Expired
    • Create task reminder at 24 hours if registration still pending via scheduled action
    • Display countdown timer showing hours remaining before auto-expiration on queue view
  • Approval Workflow & Notifications
    • Notify partner on status change via email using approval status change email templates
    • Log all approval actions to audit trail with user, timestamp, and action reason captured
    • Create linked Opportunity record upon approval with stage set to Prospecting
    • Enforce minimum partner tier of Silver required for enterprise-level deals before approval
  • Deal Protection & Exclusivity
    • Grant 6-month deal protection exclusivity from approval date on Registration__c.Protected_Until__c
    • Validate end-customer account is not flagged before approval with compliance check
    • Prevent duplicate registrations on same customer during active protection period

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • Deal_Registration__c — partner-submitted deal record
  • Opportunity — linked opportunity for approved deals
  • Account — partner account and end-customer account

Validation & Business Logic

  • Duplicate check: match on end-customer + product family within 90 days
  • SLA: 48-hour review window; auto-escalate if unactioned
  • Deal protection: 6-month exclusivity from approval date
  • Minimum partner tier of Silver required for enterprise-level deals
  • Cannot approve if end-customer account is flagged
