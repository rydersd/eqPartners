Tier Review Process
Tier reviews run quarterly. Automated recalculation fires on the 1st of the review month. PAM reviews recommendations and approves changes. Downgrades require 30-day advance notice to partner. Upgrades take effect immediately.
  1. System recalculates all partner scores
  2. PAM reviews auto-recommendations
  3. Overrides or approvals applied
  4. Tier changes trigger agreement amendment flow
  5. Partner notified via Experience Cloud + email
