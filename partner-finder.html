Summary

Public-facing partner directory at equinix.com/partners/partner-directory. This is the buyer-facing projection of data that partners manage via pm-directory-listing.html in PCP. All filterable attributes (solution area, geography, tier, certifications) are sourced from PCP — never entered separately here.

Data Sources

  • Company name, logo, tagline, description: partner-managed in PCP, immediate publish
  • Tier badge: auto-synced from Salesforce PRM on tier change
  • Certifications: auto-synced from program achievement data
  • Solution areas, geography: partner-managed in PCP, published after PSM review
  • Filter counts are live — computed from published partner data, not hardcoded

UX Notes

  • Filters are AND logic within a group (Network AND Workplace) and AND across groups (solution AND geography)
  • Tier filter is OR — selecting Gold + Silver shows partners at either tier
  • CloudBridge card is gold-bordered — this is the "featured" treatment available at Platinum tier or via PSM promotion
  • Sort options: Relevance (search-ranked), Alphabetical, Tier desc, Recently updated — defaults to Relevance
  • Active filter pills are removable without touching the sidebar checkboxes
  • Certifications filter group collapsed by default — most buyers don't filter on this
  • Load more pagination (not infinite scroll) — preserves filter state on back navigation
