✦
Agentforce generates the executive brief at Step 4 — automatically triggered when an escalation is classified as Emergency. Template ID: PTMPL-ESCALATION-BRIEF-V2 · Setup path: Setup → Agentforce → Document Generation → Prompt Templates. The brief is stored as a separate reference document on the ESC record and in the partner account file. Human review (PSM) is mandatory before distribution — the system does not send autonomously.

⚠ Why This Became an Emergency

  • Two reps — one from Redwood, one from a competing partner — submitted deal registrations for Meridian Capital within 12 minutes of each other.
  • Standard DR review (Deal Desk) flagged the timestamp conflict but lacked clear policy to resolve it independently — escalated to PSM.
  • PSM assessment revealed this was the third commission dispute from Redwood in 18 months, and Redwood is a Gold-tier partner at risk of tier downgrade.
  • At-risk commission: $280K Meridian deal — triggering VP-level engagement threshold per Equinix partner escalation policy.
  • Without the Agentforce brief, VP would have entered the partner call without context — or the call would have been delayed 2–3 days while PSM prepared manually.

✦ What Agentforce Contributes

  • Pulls structured data from 4 Salesforce objects: Escalation__c, PartnerAccount__c, DealRegistration__c, CommissionHistory__c.
  • Generates a 5-section brief: Partner Snapshot, Dispute Details, Relationship History (12-month narrative), Talking Points (5 numbered), Resolution Path.
  • PSM adds annotations — AI narrative is preserved. Adds PSM professional judgment as a contextual overlay, not a replacement.
  • Brief is attached to the ESC record and emailed to VP Channel. Also stored in Redwood's partner account file for future reference.
  • Estimated time saved vs. manual brief preparation: 2–3 hours per escalation. At ~40 emergency escalations/year across the PSM team, this is ~100 hours recovered.

Design Implications

  • PSM screen: "Generate Brief" button on ESC record should be prominent — not buried in a related list. Pre-fill with classification context.
  • Notification gap: Brian Walsh (VP, Redwood) currently has no in-portal escalation notification. Brief is delivered by email only — a blind spot to address.
  • Deal Desk screen: Timestamp conflicts should surface a visual alert during DR review, not just a text flag. dealdesk-queue.html needs a conflict severity indicator.
  • Agentforce brief: Should be stored as an immutable document after PSM approval — not editable post-send. Version history on ESC record.
  • VP summary view: Brian Walsh identified as needing a proactive quarterly executive summary email (not a portal screen) — separate from the emergency brief flow.
  • Resolution notification: Partner rep (Steve Rogers) should receive an in-portal notification when the escalation is closed — currently no wired screen for this.
