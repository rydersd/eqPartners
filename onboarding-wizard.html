Summary

6-step post-activation account setup wizard. The user arrives here already authenticated, directly from the welcome screen (onboarding-welcome.html). No org data exists yet — only the signed agreement. This wizard establishes the partner's operational profile, team, training assignments, deal defaults, and product focus.

Flow Position

Apply → Confirmation → Verification → Agreement → Set Password → Welcome → Setup Wizard (this) → Dashboard

Skip Design

  • Every step 1–5 has a "Skip for now →" ghost button alongside the primary Continue action.
  • A persistent "Exit to dashboard →" link in the context bar is always visible.
  • Skipped steps are recorded in localStorage (pcp-onboarding-skipped) so the dashboard can show a "Continue your setup" progress banner.
  • The banner appears on the Partner Home dashboard until all steps are complete or the user explicitly dismisses it. It links back to the wizard at the first incomplete step.
  • Setup is always accessible via My Account → Getting Started.

Steps

  • Step 1 — Organization: PSM welcome banner. Fill in company profile (pre-populated from application where available). DUNS and Tax ID optional. Contact details with read-only email.
  • Step 2 — Your Team: Invite team members with role assignments. Logged-in user pinned at top. Invitations queue until Step 6 is reached or the user exits.
  • Step 3 — Training: Role-based learning paths shown per team member (derived from Step 2 role assignments). Individual courses can be removed or added. Training is sent with portal invitations.
  • Step 4 — Deals & Marketing: How deal registration works (3-step flow), currency default, deal alert toggle. MDF overview with typical allocation, co-marketing program list, MDF alert toggle.
  • Step 5 — Programs & Preferences: Equinix product focus (2-col cards), target verticals, regions (checkbox grids). Notification toggles. Default deal owner.
  • Step 6 — You're Ready: Completion state. Required next steps: onboarding training, agreement signing, PSM kickoff, first deal registration.

Training Step — Role Mapping

  • Portal Admin / Sales Ops: Partner Central Fundamentals (Required), Team Management & Permissions (Required), Deal Workflow & Approval Queues (Recommended), Reporting & Analytics (Optional)
  • Account Executive / Deal Reg User: Deal Registration Mastery (Required), Equinix Products Essentials (Required), Partner Co-Sell Playbook (Recommended), Competitive Landscape (Optional)
  • Technical: Equinix Technical Foundations (Required), Interconnection & Fabric Deep Dive (Recommended), API & Integration Essentials (Optional)
  • Finance Contact: Commission & Revenue Overview (Required), MDF Claims Process (Recommended)
  • In production, these are dynamically generated based on the role values assigned in Step 2.

Design Notes

  • Post-auth header (dark pc-header) used — user is authenticated
  • PSM intro card is dark and prominent to personalize from the first screen
  • DUNS and Tax ID are optional at setup, flagged as needed for MDF — reduces friction without blocking completion
  • Team invitations queue until Step 6 to avoid sending premature credentials
  • Training paths are generated from Step 2 role selections; the step shows a static demo until Step 2 data is committed
  • Deals & Marketing step is primarily orientation — only the currency default and two notification toggles require input
  • Programs & Preferences step covers product/vertical/region focus (previously Step 3) merged with notifications (previously Step 4)
