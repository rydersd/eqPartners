pam-tier-dashboard.html

PAM Portal · Alex Rivera · Tier Distribution Dashboard

Job to Be Done

"When managing the partner program, I want a program-wide view of tier health so I can intervene before demotions happen and surface upgrade candidates proactively — not reactively after the review date."

Screen Purpose

Program-level oversight for Alex. While partner-financial.html shows one partner at a time, this screen shows all 42 partners as a portfolio — who's at risk, who's eligible to upgrade, and how the tier mix is shifting quarter over quarter.

Key Design Decisions

  • Two separate tables: at-risk vs. upgrade — mixing them creates action confusion. Demotion risk and upgrade opportunity call for completely different interventions (MDF push vs. recognition/incentive)
  • Revenue gap shown as dollar amount, not percentage — Alex needs to know whether a gap is closeable in the remaining window, and $220K reads faster than "88% of threshold"
  • MDF utilization shown inline — low MDF utilization + revenue gap is the diagnostic signal for intervention; showing both together confirms the pattern
  • "Intervene" vs. "Review" button distinction — red-chip partners with <45 days show "Intervene" (higher urgency framing); others show "Review" (routine check)
  • Bar chart for tier movement history — upgrades/demotions over time is a strategic metric Alex reports to leadership; the chart provides context the KPI tiles don't

Data Model

  • Account: Partner_Tier__c, Tier_Review_Date__c, Partner_Type__c, PAM__c
  • Partner_Revenue__c: Total_ARR__c rolled up from closed-won Opportunities with Partner_Account__c set
  • Tier_Threshold__mdt: Tier__c, Min_ARR__c, Min_Certs__c — configurable tier boundaries
  • MDF_Allocation__c: Utilization_Pct__c (formula: Spent / Allocated)
  • Tier_Change_History__c: Account__c, Previous_Tier__c, New_Tier__c, Change_Date__c, Change_Type__c (Upgrade/Demotion)

Business Logic

  • At-risk threshold: Revenue_Gap__c > 0 AND Tier_Review_Date__c ≤ TODAY() + 60
  • Upgrade candidate: Revenue_Gap_To_Next__c < (10% of next tier threshold) AND trajectory = positive
  • MDF utilization warning: Utilization_Pct__c < 60% within 90 days of fiscal year end
  • "Intervene" CTA fires when review is ≤ 45 days; "Review" when 46–90 days
