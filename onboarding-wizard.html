Summary

8-step post-activation account setup wizard. User arrives already authenticated from onboarding-welcome.html. This wizard establishes the partner's security posture, operational profile, agreement, tax/banking, team, training, and preferences.

Flow Position

Apply → Confirmation → Review → Agreement → Auth Setup → Welcome → Setup Wizard (this) → pm-home-day0

Steps

  • Step 1 — Security: MFA setup. Choose method (app, SMS, hardware key). TOTP verification for app method. Required before deal submissions.
  • Step 2 — Org Profile: PSM welcome banner. Company details (pre-populated from application). Contact details with read-only email.
  • Step 3 — Contract: Review PPA summary. DocuSign CTA. After signing → pending countersign state. User can continue to step 4 or exit to dashboard. Dashboard shows resume banner while pending.
  • Step 4 — Tax & Banking: EIN, VAT, DUNS, W-9. Banking details for MDF reimbursements. Gated note: not processed until agreement is fully executed.
  • Step 5 — Team: Invite members with role assignments. Jordan pinned at top. Invitations queue until wizard completes.
  • Step 6 — Training: Role-based learning paths per team member from step 5.
  • Step 7 — Deals & Preferences: Deal registration how-it-works. Currency + deal owner defaults. Product/region focus. Notification toggles.
  • Step 8 — Complete: Summary checklist. MFA and countersign shown as pending if not done. CTA to dashboard.

Contract Pending Design

  • After Jordan clicks "Sign with DocuSign", the sign state is replaced with the pending countersign banner.
  • She can proceed to steps 4-8 while waiting. Tax & banking data is accepted but flagged as pending processing.
  • If she exits to dashboard, pcp-contract-pending=1 is set in localStorage. The day-0 dashboard reads this and shows a resume banner with a non-actionable "Equinix is reviewing your agreement" sub-state.
  • When countersign is complete: email notification + portal notification. The dashboard banner updates to "Agreement signed — continue your setup" with a CTA back to step 4.
  • Demo: 15-second timer on pm-home-day0.html simulates the countersign notification arriving.

MFA Design

  • MFA is step 1 of the wizard — Jordan encounters it as the first action before any org data is touched.
  • Three method options: Authenticator app (recommended), SMS, Hardware key.
  • App flow shows QR code wireframe + manual entry key + 6-digit verification code inputs.
  • MFA can be skipped but is flagged as "Required" in the completion step checklist and on the day-0 dashboard (persistent nag banner).
  • Invited users (rep flow) encounter MFA in pm-first-login.html instead.

Skip / Exit Design

  • Every step 1–7 has a "Skip for now →" ghost button.
  • Context bar shows persistent "Exit to dashboard →" at all steps.
  • Skipped steps tracked in localStorage (pcp-onboarding-skipped).
  • Resume banner on day-0 dashboard links back to first incomplete step.
  • Always accessible via My Account → Getting Started.
