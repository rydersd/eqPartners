Summary

5-step post-authentication onboarding wizard. Runs immediately after auth setup on first login. Establishes the partner's operational defaults (profile, team, product focus, notification preferences) and surfaces the required training and agreement actions before first deal registration.

Flow Position

Registration chain: Apply → Confirmation → Acceptance Email → Auth Setup → Onboarding Wizard (this) → Partner Central Dashboard

Steps

  • Step 1 — Profile: Welcome banner (PSM intro), review/confirm org data from registration, add DUNS/Tax ID, confirm contact details. Pre-filled with CloudBridge data.
  • Step 2 — Your Team: Invite team members, assign portal roles. Registered user shown as "You" with locked role. Invites queued until onboarding completes.
  • Step 3 — Programs: Select Equinix products, verticals, and regions. Drives PSM resource targeting and portal content personalization.
  • Step 4 — Preferences: Toggle notifications per category. Set deal currency and default deal owner. All toggles default to most useful state based on partner type.
  • Step 5 — Get Started: Completion state. Checklist with required items (training, agreement, kickoff) and first deal registration link.

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Dark welcome banner with PSM avatar, name, role, and direct email
  • Profile form: pre-filled from Salesforce Account record, DUNS/Tax editable
  • Team table: registered user pinned at top (locked), invite row below existing members
  • Product cards (2-col): checkbox + title + description; verticals and regions as 3-col checkbox grids
  • Toggle rows for 6 notification types + deal defaults (currency, owner)
  • Completion checklist: Done/Required/Upcoming states with direct action links

Interactive Elements

  • Stepper nav (click to jump only if step already visited)
  • Profile form inputs — editable over pre-filled (tinted) values
  • Role selects in team table; Add Member form + "+" button
  • Remove (×) button removes team row
  • Product checkboxes, vertical/region checkbox grids
  • Notification toggle switches
  • Currency / deal owner selects
  • CTA links in checklist (training, agreement, schedule, deal reg)
  • Go to Dashboard CTA

Functionality

  • Wizard state persists per session; user can close and resume from last completed step
  • Step 1 profile saves to Salesforce Contact/Account on Save & Continue
  • Step 2 team invites queued; invitation emails sent on final step completion
  • Step 3 product selections write to partner profile record; drive PSM dashboard segmentation
  • Step 5 checklist items link to existing portal pages; completion status tracked
  • Agreement sign-off gates deal registration until complete
  • Training completion tracked via LMS integration

Design Notes

  • Post-auth header (dark pc-header) used here — user is now logged in
  • PSM intro card is dark/prominent to personalize the experience from the first screen
  • DUNS and Tax ID are optional at onboarding but flagged as needed for MDF — reduces friction without blocking completion
  • Team invites queue until Step 5 completion to avoid sending premature access emails
  • Checkbox grids follow the <10 options tile pattern; product cards use a 2-col variation to allow longer descriptions
  • Toggle defaults set based on partner type: Reseller gets deal and co-sell on; weekly digest off by default
  • "Back" nav available on all steps but doesn't require save confirmation — wizard saves progressively
