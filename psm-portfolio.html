psm-portfolio.html

PSM Portal · Marcus Chen · Partner Portfolio

Job to Be Done

"When I start my week, I want a single view of every partner's health score, pipeline status, training gaps, and upcoming QBR date — so I know exactly where to focus my time before I open a single email."

Screen Purpose

Marcus's home base. The portfolio card view gives him a fast gestalt read on all 8 assigned partners: who's healthy, who needs intervention, and what specific actions are required. The priority section removes any ambiguity about what to do first.

Key Design Decisions

  • Color-coded left border on cards — green/yellow/red at a glance, without reading any numbers. Most of Marcus's morning review is visual triage
  • Health score visible in header — composite score (pipeline, training, engagement, deal activity) gives Marcus a single metric for stakeholder conversations: "CloudBridge is at 68 — I need another 12 points before the QBR"
  • Priority Actions section — distills the portfolio into the 3 things that must happen this week. This is the first thing Marcus reads
  • 4-metric grid per card — pipeline, deal count, training progress, and QBR date are the four recurring questions Marcus answers in every check-in with leadership
  • Context-sensitive actions — TerraCloud shows "Enroll in Training" because training = 0/3. Vertex shows "Resolve Dispute" because there's an open dispute. Cards show the right action for the partner's situation

Health Score Components

  • Pipeline coverage: 30 pts (active pipeline vs. quarterly quota)
  • Training completion: 25 pts (completed modules / required modules)
  • Engagement: 20 pts (QBR recency, portal logins, deal registrations last 30 days)
  • Deal activity: 25 pts (active deals, recent wins, deal registration frequency)
