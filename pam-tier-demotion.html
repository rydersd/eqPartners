Design Notes — pam-tier-demotion.html

Purpose

Closes the gap identified in the at-risk panel on pam-home-dashboard.html. Alex flagged TerraCloud as at-risk; this is the screen where she actually pulls the trigger on demotion. Previously, the wireframe had no downstream flow from "at-risk."

Design Decisions

Two-step action

Alex can send a warning first (without demoting) or initiate the full demotion. Most programs have a cure period — the warning option reflects that. Forcing a demotion without the warning step would likely violate partner contract terms.

Checkbox confirmation gate

The confirmation checkbox requires Alex to affirm that Marcus has been consulted and a regional lead has approved. This prevents unilateral PAM demotions without cross-functional review — a real governance risk in partner programs.

Impact summary split: immediate vs. unchanged

Partners need to know what changes immediately and what stays the same. Showing "existing deal registrations protected through expiry" under "unchanged" reduces panic calls from the partner's AE.

Partner-Facing Counterpart

When Alex initiates this demotion, Jordan Reeves at TerraCloud sees pm-tier-appeal.html — the appeal screen. The 30-day window is stated both in the notification message and surfaced in Jordan's portal.
