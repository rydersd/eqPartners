Salesforce Objects & Fields
Deal_Registration__c: Deal Reg ID, Account ID (lookup), Opportunity (lookup), Amount__c, Product__c, Stage__c (Opportunity stage), Commission_Rate__c, Equinix_AE__c (lookup to User)
Opportunity: Opp ID, Account Name, Amount, StageName (Proposal/Quote/Order), CloseDate, Deal_Registration__c (lookup)
Partner_Commission__c: Commission ID, Deal_Registration__c (lookup), Amount__c, Rate__c, Commission_Earned__c, Payment_Status__c (Pending/Paid/Disputed), Payment_Date__c
Architectural Decision: MRR-Only Commission Model

Decision: Partner commissions are calculated exclusively on MRR (Monthly Recurring Revenue) — the contracted monthly value of colocation, cross-connects, and managed services. Non-Recurring Revenue (NRR) such as installation charges, smart hands, custom buildouts, and migration fees are excluded from commission calculations.

Rationale: NRR is typically low-margin cost-recovery for Equinix (sending a tech to rack a server, one-time site prep). Including NRR in commission complicates reporting without meaningfully driving partner behavior. MRR is the metric that aligns partner incentives with Equinix's recurring revenue model and drives the right deal-making behavior — larger, longer-term service contracts.

Commission formula: Commission_Earned__c = Deal MRR × Commission_Rate__c (tier-based: Bronze 8%, Silver 12%, Gold 15%). Co-sell bonus (+2%) applies when Equinix AE is co-assigned.

All revenue KPIs on this page reference MRR: Total Revenue, Commission Earned YTD, Pending Commission, Pipeline Value, and per-deal Amount columns all reflect monthly recurring values. NRR line items (installation, setup fees) appear on invoices but are not surfaced in partner portal revenue reporting.

Validation Rules & Edge Cases
  • PM sees all deals from their org — not just their own submissions
  • Commission visible only after payment processing (month-end cycle)
  • Deal registration requires PM or Rep role permission
  • Commission_Rate__c defaults to Account.Partner_Tier__c's rate (15% for Gold)
  • Co-Sell bonus (+2%) applied if Partner_Relationship__c exists on Opportunity
  • Pipeline value excludes Closed Lost opportunities
Error States
  • "No deals found — register your first deal"
  • "Commission calculation pending — check back after month-end processing (last Friday of month)"
  • "Deal registration failed — customer account not found"
  • "Commission dispute detected — contact your PSM"
Accessibility Notes
  • Funnel chart uses aria-label to describe stage progression
  • Deal tables sortable with keyboard navigation (arrow keys)
  • Commission values use aria-label (e.g., "Commission: $127,500")
  • Status badges use color + text + aria-label
  • Tier comparison section uses proper heading hierarchy
User Flow (Numbered Steps)
  1. PM reviews KPI cards (pipeline, closed won, pending commission)
  2. PM checks funnel visualization to see deal stages
  3. PM reviews active deals table, sorted by stage
  4. PM identifies deals needing follow-up (pending approval, quote stage)
  5. PM clicks deal name to view details or update status
  6. PM checks commission summary table to see earnings from closed deals
  7. PM identifies top-performing reps by deal volume
  8. PM registers new deal or follows up on pending deals

Design Notes

Summary

The Deals & Revenue dashboard provides Equinix leadership with visibility into partner-sourced revenue, pipeline contribution, and performance trends across regions, partners, and deal stages.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Monitor partner revenue contributions and pipeline velocity
  • Track deal revenue against partner tier agreements and targets
  • Identify top-performing partners and revenue drivers
  • Forecast quarterly and annual revenue totals
  • Analyze revenue trends and growth patterns

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • KPI cards: Total Closed Revenue, Pipeline Value, Forecast, vs. Target
  • Top partners by revenue list with sparkline trends
  • Deal-level revenue breakdown table with stage and close date
  • Quarter-over-quarter revenue comparison chart
  • Revenue pipeline forecast by stage and month
  • Filter controls for date range, partner tier, region, deal stage

Interactive Elements

  • Click partner name to drill down to detailed deal breakdown
  • Hover over chart data points for exact values and details
  • Toggle between deal view and partner aggregated view
  • Export revenue data to CSV for external reporting
  • Download as PDF for stakeholder presentations
  • Drill down from quarter to individual deals with links

Functionality

  • Data Integration & Aggregation
    • Salesforce Opportunity/Deal query aggregating closed-won revenue by partner and period
    • Revenue recognition on close date with automatic data refresh and hourly sync
  • Calculation & Reporting
    • Calculate month/quarter/year revenue totals with automatic comparison to prior periods
    • Revenue-vs-target variance calculation per partner agreement term to identify performance gaps
  • Forecasting & Filtering
    • Pipeline forecast: Apply 80% probability weighting to open-stage deals for conservative projections
    • Partner tier-based filtering with automatic exclusion of expired Partner_Agreement__c records

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • Opportunity: Amount, CloseDate, StageName, Account_Partner__c, Revenue_Recognition_Date__c
  • Partner_Agreement__c: Account, Revenue_Target__c, Term_Start_Date__c, Term_End_Date__c

Validation & Business Logic

  • Revenue figures pull from closed-won (Order) stage deals only
  • Revenue recognized on deal close date, not forecast close date
  • Partner tier filters exclude expired agreements
  • Forecast calculations use 80% probability for open pipeline deals
