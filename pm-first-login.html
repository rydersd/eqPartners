Invite Your Team

Add reps and other users to your org. Each person you invite gets their own login and lands in the Rep Portal with role-appropriate access. You can invite one at a time or send bulk invitations by uploading a CSV.

  • Resend or revoke invitations at any time from Team Management

Assign Training & Certifications

Every rep you invite starts with a default training track based on their role. You can customize assignments, add or remove courses, and set due dates. Certifications earned by your team count toward your org's tier status.

  • Required courses auto-assign when you create a user — portal handles it for you
  • Add supplemental courses from the catalog to any rep at any time
  • Set or extend due dates on individual assignments
  • Track completion rates per person on the Training Dashboard
  • Certifications (e.g., Equinix Interconnect Certified) appear on rep profiles and contribute to tier advancement

Your Partnership Tier & Benefits

CloudBridge Solutions is currently a Bronze Partner. Your tier determines the benefits, deal registration commission rates, MDF budget, and co-selling resources your org can access. Here's how the tiers compare:

Tier Min. Revenue Deal Reg Uplift MDF Budget PSM Access
● Bronze ← You are here $0 +5% $2,500 Shared queue
● Silver $250K ARR +10% $10,000 Named PSM
● Gold $750K ARR +15% $25,000 Named PSM + SE
● Platinum $2M ARR +20% $75,000 Dedicated team

Tier is re-evaluated quarterly. Training completions, active certifications, and deal volume all factor into tier advancement. Your PSM can provide a roadmap to Silver.

Deal Registration & Pipeline

Your reps register deals through the Rep Portal. You see every deal in your org's pipeline here, can track approval status, and co-register on behalf of a rep when needed.

  • Deals move through: Draft → Submitted → Under Review → Approved / Rejected
  • Approved deals lock the commission uplift for your org — timing matters
  • Submit a deal on behalf of a rep from the Deals tab in this portal
  • Revenue and commissions roll up on your Partnership Health dashboard
  • Expired deals (no activity in 90 days) auto-close — set reminders for your team
Go to Deals & Revenue →
💰
Market Development Funds (MDF)

As a Bronze Partner, CloudBridge Solutions has $2,500 in MDF available for co-marketing activities. MDF can be used for events, digital campaigns, co-branded materials, or customer demos. You submit a request, Equinix approves it, and you get reimbursed after submitting proof of performance.

  • Submit an MDF request with activity description, expected reach, and budget breakdown
  • Approval typically takes 5–7 business days; your PSM can expedite
  • After the activity, submit a Proof of Performance (PoP) to receive reimbursement
  • Unused MDF does not roll over — plan activities early in each quarter

Design Specs

Summary

First-login onboarding wizard for the Partner Admin user — the first person from a partner org to activate their account after the partner application is approved. Jordan Reeves (jordan.reeves@cloudbridge.io), Portal Admin at CloudBridge Solutions. Five-step wizard: Welcome → Security Setup → Profile → Set Up Your Team (admin orientation) → Ready to Go. The portal orientation step is the differentiator from the Rep first-login: it covers team invitations, training assignment, tier economics, deal registration concepts, and MDF access.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Orient a new partner admin to their admin role and responsibilities
  • Enable immediate team-building (queue rep invitations before leaving setup)
  • Explain tier mechanics so the admin understands what drives advancement
  • Surface deal registration and MDF at a conceptual level — not overwhelming, just enough to know it exists
  • Connect admin to their PSM and support resources

Design Specification

Step 4 — Set Up Your Team (Admin-Specific)

  • Five orientation blocks, each collapsible: Invite Team, Assign Training, Tier Benefits, Deal Registration, MDF
  • Inline quick-invite form: email + role dropdown + add-to-queue. Tagged list shows queued invites. Invitations send on wizard completion.
  • Tier table: 4 tiers × 5 columns (Min Revenue, Deal Reg Uplift, MDF Budget, PSM Access). Current row highlighted.
  • Each section links to its respective portal page for deeper exploration
  • Save & Finish Later preserves wizard state in User.Onboarding_Step__c

Navigation

  • Nav links greyed and aria-disabled until onboarding completes
  • Active step pulsing ring on step-number circle
  • Completed steps show ✓ check (font-size:0 + ::after content trick)

Persona & Routing

  • Persona badge: persona-badge--pm (Partner Manager Admin)
  • Portal: Experience Cloud — PM Portal
  • On wizard completion: User.IsFirstLogin__c=false → redirect to pm-home-dashboard.html
  • Queued invitations trigger bulk Contact+User provisioning flow on completion

Salesforce Objects

  • User: IsFirstLogin__c, MFA_Verified__c, Portal_Role__c = 'Admin'
  • Onboarding_Step__c: Step_Order__c, Completed__c, Skipped__c (same as rep)
  • Pending_Invitation__c: Inviter_User__c, Email__c, Role__c, Status__c, Sent_Date__c
  • Partner_Account__c: Tier__c, MDF_Balance__c, Revenue_YTD__c
  • Provisioning flow: on wizard complete, batch-process Pending_Invitation__c WHERE Status='Queued'

Accessibility Notes

  • Tier table uses scope="col" on headers and role="table" + aria-label
  • Invite tag × controls have aria-label="Remove" and role="button" + keyboard handler
  • Greyed nav links carry aria-disabled="true" and tabindex="-1"
  • Progress bar: role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow/min/max
  • Active step has aria-current="step"
