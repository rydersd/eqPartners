Inventory-First Architecture: The Service Inventory section is now organized by metro location (DC5, SV5, NY9) rather than a flat asset table. This reflects how Acme Corporation thinks about their infrastructure—location-first. Each metro group displays cabinets, cross-connects, and edge devices co-located at that facility, with power utilization bars showing capacity health. Customers can quickly assess their footprint at each metro and identify expansion opportunities (e.g., SV5 at 80% utilization triggers an expansion recommendation).

Monthly Burn Rate with Sparkline Trajectory: The KPI cards have been revised to surface financial planning data. The "Monthly Burn Rate: $47.5K" card includes a 6-month sparkline ($42.0K to $47.5K) that gives financial decision-makers instant visual context on cost trajectory. Paired with the "Projected Annual Spend: $570K" card, customers can budget confidently and plan for cost escalation. This replaces the generic "Contract Utilization: 78%" metric with actionable financial insights.

Orders Requiring Attention (Outlier-First): The order view has been flipped. Outlier orders—those delayed, stuck, pending approval, or missing milestones—are now displayed first with high-priority urgency badges. Example: ORD-2025-0156 (1 week behind schedule) and ORD-2025-0189 (waiting 5 days, SLA 3 days) appear at the top for immediate action. Completed orders (6 months audit trail) are collapsed below in a secondary section, reducing cognitive load while preserving historical visibility.

Data Integrity & Salesforce Foundation: All changes are backed by Salesforce objects (Asset, Order, OrderItem, ServiceContract, Product2, PricebookEntry, Account). Row-level security ensures Acme Corporation customers see only their own assets and orders. The page maintains WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility with keyboard navigation, screen reader support, and semantic HTML. Milestones, power utilization bars, and urgency indicators are all data-driven and live-updated via Salesforce automations (Asset_Provisioning_Flow, Order_Status_Notification_Flow, Power_Utilization_Alert_Flow).

Design Specs

Summary

Comprehensive order and service management dashboard enabling customers to track active provisioning, monitor costs and utilization, and identify at-risk orders requiring immediate attention.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Visualize service costs and project annual spend trends
  • Identify orders at risk (delayed, pending approval) for management attention
  • Track provisioning progress through milestone timeline
  • Monitor power and space utilization per metro for capacity planning
  • Review asset inventory and individual service details by location

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Service Inventory KPIs: total assets, active metros, monthly burn rate, projected annual spend
  • Orders Requiring Attention section with delayed/pending approval orders highlighted
  • Expansion panels showing order details, items, and provisioning milestones
  • Completed Orders collapsible section with historical 6-month view
  • Service Inventory by Metro with power utilization bars and asset tables per location
  • Metro-level details: cabinets, cross-connects, power usage, capacity ratios

Interactive Elements

  • Expand/collapse outlier order panels to view full details and milestones
  • Export service inventory button for data analysis
  • New Service Request button to initiate orders
  • Collapsible Completed Orders section to hide historical data
  • Click asset IDs to drill into service configuration details

Functionality

  • Order & Provisioning Management
    • Order status automation pulling provisioning state from fulfillment workflow via middleware callout updating Order.Status field from fulfillment system state codes
    • Milestone tracking on Order records with due date calculations based on Order.Created_Date__c plus Service_SLA__c (in days) formula field
    • Delay detection logic comparing Order.Actual_Completion_Date__c to Order.Expected_Completion_Date__c triggering escalation email if delta exceeds 2 days
  • Financial Analytics & Cost Tracking
    • Cost tracking on Order and Asset records with monthly burn rate calculation using SOQL rollup summary fields summing monthly charges
    • Trend sparkline generation via custom metric calculation storing prior 12 months of monthly charges in Order.Monthly_Charges_JSON__c field for charting
    • Discrepancy alerts flagging when Order.Total_Cost__c deviates more than 10% from original estimate via formula field and workflow alert
  • Capacity & Risk Management
    • Power and space utilization calculations aggregated by metro using SOQL GROUP BY queries summing Asset.Power_Draw_W__c and Asset.Rack_Units_Used__c per metro
    • Utilization thresholds comparing aggregated values against Account-level contracted capacity stored in Account.Total_Power_W__c and Account.Total_RU__c
    • At-risk order flagging based on deadline deviation using formula field when Order.Days_Until_Due__c is less than 7 days and Order.Status NOT IN ('Completed', 'Cancelled')
  • Security & Access Control
    • Row-level security (RLS) enforcement at Account level ensuring logged-in customer user sees only Assets and Orders related to their Account via OrgWideDefault setting
    • Sharing rule cascading Asset permissions from parent Account_Share__c records preventing cross-account data visibility
    • Sensitive cost and margin fields using field-level security to hide from customer user profile while showing account team members

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • Asset — Represents each purchased service (cabinets, cross-connects, Network Edge instances); linked to Account and related to Order via order fulfillment
  • Order — Captures order history, status tracking, and provisioning workflows
  • Account — Customer account data (Acme Corporation, EQ-2024-0451) with Account-level Row-Level Security (RLS)
