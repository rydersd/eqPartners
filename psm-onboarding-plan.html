Design Specs

Summary

30-60-90 day onboarding plan tracker for PSM to monitor partner milestones, surface blockers, and ensure timely completion of onboarding phase requirements. Featuring Peter Parker (CloudBridge Solutions, Junior Sales Rep, joined Jan 30), BIZ track, target completion Apr 30, 2026, assigned PSM Marcus Chen.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Provides PSM visibility into structured 30-60-90 day onboarding journey with clear phase milestones and dependencies
  • Surfaces blockers and overdue items to enable proactive intervention (e.g., rescheduling certification exams)
  • Tracks milestone completion across three phases with target vs actual date comparison
  • Maintains detailed activity log for compliance documentation and partner communication reference
  • Supports QBR preparation with comprehensive onboarding status and achievement metrics

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Partner header with company name, tier
  • Overall progress bar showing Day 42 of 90 with On Track status
  • Blockers & Overdue Milestones alert section flagging Product Certification ex...
  • Three-column timeline grid
  • Detailed Milestone Tracking section with Completed Activities and In Progress...
  • Recent Activity timeline with 4 dated entries

Interactive Elements

  • View Resources button to access onboarding materials and templates
  • Update Progress button to mark milestones complete and log activities
  • Expandable milestone items in timeline cards showing status indicators and de...
  • Color-coded timeline cards for visual status tracking

Functionality

  • Onboarding Plan & Milestone Retrieval
    • Fetch Onboarding_Plan__c record for specific partner with related Onboarding_Milestone__c child records
    • Query Onboarding_Milestone__c ordered by Phase__c (Phase 1, 2, 3) and Target_Date__c sequence
    • Display milestone title, description, owner, and current completion status indicator
  • Progress Tracking & Timeline Metrics
    • Calculate days elapsed as (TODAY() - Plan_Start_Date__c) and phase duration based on Plan_Type__c (30/60/90 day plans)
    • Display percentage completion by counting Completed_Milestones__c / Total_Milestones__c
    • Show timeline progress bar with visual completion status and days remaining to plan end date
  • Overdue & Risk Detection
    • Identify overdue milestones where Target_Date__c < TODAY() and Status__c != Complete
    • Flag in alert banner with count of overdue items and recommended actions
    • Trigger escalation notification when phase delay > 7 days to alert PSM for intervention
  • Activity Logging & History
    • Log milestone completion to AuditTrail__c record with timestamp, user, and completion notes
    • Track task completion status changes and maintain historical record for trending analysis
    • Display activity timeline showing when each milestone was completed and by whom
  • Onboarding Metrics & Reporting
    • Generate onboarding completion metrics (% complete, on-track vs. delayed) for QBR discussions
    • Calculate average time per phase to benchmark partner velocity against company baseline
    • Create summary report for partner communications showing progress, next steps, and recommended actions
