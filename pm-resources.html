Frequently Asked Questions
How do I submit a deal registration? +
Go to the Deals section and click "Submit New Deal Registration". Fill in the customer details, estimated amount, and expected close date. Your PSM will review within 24 hours. Once approved, the deal is protected from channel conflict.
What's the timeline for MDF claim reimbursement? +
Claims are typically processed within 5-7 business days. Once approved, payment is issued on the last business day of the month. You can view the status of all claims in the Marketing & MDF section.
How much MDF do I receive at each tier? +
Bronze Tier: $10,000 annually | Silver Tier: $25,000 annually | Gold Tier: $50,000 annually. MDF resets each calendar year and cannot be carried over. Unused MDF does not roll to the following year.
What are the commission rates by tier? +
Bronze: 8% of deal value | Silver: 12% of deal value | Gold: 15% of deal value. Commission is calculated when the Opportunity is marked "Closed Won". Accelerator bonuses apply for exceeding quarterly targets.
How do I advance to the next tier? +
Complete required training courses, achieve sales targets, and demonstrate customer satisfaction. Check your tier progress on the Home dashboard—it shows exactly which courses and milestones are required for the next tier.
Can I access co-branded marketing assets? +
Yes! Visit the Marketing & MDF section to access our Co-Branded Asset Library. Logos, templates, pitch decks, case studies, and webinar slides are available for download without pre-approval.
What training is required for my team? +
All team members must complete the 6 required foundational courses: Security Fundamentals, Compliance 101, Cloud Solutions, Advanced Networking, Sales Excellence, and Advanced Security. See the Training page for enrollment details and deadlines.
How are leads shared with partners? +
Qualified leads are routed to partners based on geographic territory and product specialization. Lead acceptance goes through your PSM. Your dashboard shows all shared leads and conversion metrics for ROI tracking.
Who is my Partner Success Manager? +
Your PSM information is displayed in the Support card on this page. They're your primary point of contact for program questions, training support, deal guidance, and tier advancement. You can reach them directly via email or schedule a call.
How do I request custom marketing assets? +
Submit a request through the "Request Custom Asset" button in the Marketing section. Specify your needs, timeline, and budget. Our marketing team will assess feasibility and work with your PSM on execution.

© 2026 Equinix Partner Manager Portal · Wireframe specification

Design Notes

Summary

Centralized resource library providing partners access to program documentation, sales enablement materials, FAQs, and support contacts. Enables quick discovery and sharing of relevant resources across categories.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Access partner program resources and collateral
  • Search for marketing and sales enablement materials
  • Find product documentation and training content
  • Locate co-sell and positioning resources
  • Share resources with partner teams

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Resource library organized by category (Getting Started, Sales Enablement, Support)
  • Search bar with keyword search and autocomplete
  • Resource cards with title, description, preview thumbnail, and type badge
  • Filter options by resource type, program, partner tier, and language
  • Recently updated resources list with metadata
  • Featured/recommended resources carousel
  • Download, preview, and sharing action buttons per resource

Interactive Elements

  • Search resources by keyword across title and description
  • Filter by resource type, program, access level, and tier
  • Sort results by date added, relevance, or popularity
  • Preview resource content before downloading
  • Download resource or request access workflow
  • Share resource via email or copy link to clipboard
  • Add/remove resources from personal favorites library

Functionality

  • Search & Indexing
    • Index Resource__c records in full-text search with title, description, and keywords for fast discovery
    • Query and sort recent updates by LastModifiedDate with notification flag for new content
    • Support keyword filtering across resource categories and metadata fields
  • Access Control & Tier Management
    • Apply access control via Resource_Access__c based on user partner tier and program enrollment
    • Validate user permissions before displaying tier-specific resources and restricted content
    • Track user authentication status for access logging and audit trails
  • Resource Retrieval & Display
    • Auto-generate resource thumbnails from first document page for preview capabilities
    • Retrieve Resource__c records with file URLs, type badges, and metadata for display
    • Support custom resource tags and allow users to curate personal collections
  • Analytics & Tracking
    • Track resource downloads and usage analytics via FileShare audit logs and event tracking
    • Monitor resource popularity metrics and generate usage reports per resource and user
    • Log file share events for compliance and performance monitoring

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • Resource__c: Title__c, Description__c, File_URL__c, Resource_Type__c, Partner_Tier__c, Publish_Date__c, Tags__c
  • Resource_Access__c: Resource__c, User__c, Access_Level__c, Shared_Date__c

Validation & Business Logic

  • Resources require published status before visibility
  • Access restricted by partner tier and program enrollment
  • File URLs must be validated and active
  • Usage tracking requires user login authentication
