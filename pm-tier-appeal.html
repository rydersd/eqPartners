Design Notes — pm-tier-appeal.html

Purpose

The partner-facing counterpart to pam-tier-demotion.html. When Alex initiates a demotion, Jordan (TerraCloud's Admin) lands here — either via email link or via a notification in the portal. This is where the partner sees exactly what Equinix measured, why they're being demoted, and how to contest it.

Design Decisions

Countdown clock

The 22-day countdown creates appropriate urgency without panic. Partners who don't see this screen until day 20 can still act. Partners who see it on day 1 know they have time to gather evidence properly.

Tier stays Gold during appeal

This is stated explicitly in the demotion notice and in step 1 of the timeline. This prevents the support ticket cascade of "we need to know if we should still use Gold-tier MDF while the appeal is pending." Answer: yes, you can.

Appeal basis dropdown

Attribution dispute is the most common legitimate appeal reason — a deal that closed through the partner gets credited to an Equinix direct AE. Without this option prominently listed, partners write angry open-text appeals that are hard to route. The dropdown routes them to the right review workflow on the PAM side.

Re-qualification timeline

The "re-qualify any quarter" point reduces churn. Partners who know demotion isn't permanent are less likely to exit the program after one bad quarter.

Gaps / Open Questions

  • [A] "Tier stays Gold during appeal" — needs legal/program confirmation. Some programs freeze tier during appeal rather than maintaining current tier.
  • [?] Is there an escalation path beyond the PAM if Jordan disagrees with Alex's appeal decision? (Recommend: yes — regional PAM lead or ombudsman)
  • [?] Can Jordan see the specific Salesforce records Equinix used to calculate ARR, or only the totals?
