Design Notes

Summary

Records and documents quarterly business review meetings with partners including discussion notes, performance metrics, and action item commitments. PSMs use this record to track partnership progress, maintain accountability, and prepare for future QBR meetings.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Document quarterly business review (QBR) outcomes and commitments
  • Track action items and follow-ups from partner meetings
  • Record partner feedback and satisfaction scores
  • Maintain historical QBR records for partner relationship history
  • Share QBR summaries with stakeholders
  • Monitor partner health trends across multiple QBRs

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • QBR header: Partner account name, meeting date, next QBR scheduled date
  • Partner Scorecard metrics: Health Score, Engagement Score, Growth Score, and Key Performance Indicators (Revenue, Training Completion, Certification Progress)
  • Action Items table: Item description, owner, due date, status (Not Started, In Progress, Completed)
  • Meeting Attendees list showing name and role for each participant
  • Meeting Notes & Minutes narrative sections covering Opening Remarks, Revenue Discussion, Training & Enablement, MDF Utilization, Deal Registration & Pipeline, Next Steps
  • Button actions for Export as PDF, Email to Attendees, Schedule Next QBR
  • Right rail Activity/Agentforce tabs showing QBR completion timeline and AI recommendations

Interactive Elements

  • Expand/collapse sections within Meeting Notes
  • Mark action items as complete with checkbox in table
  • Status badge updates: Not Started, In Progress, Completed (with color coding)
  • Export as PDF button to download QBR document
  • Email to Attendees button to send meeting minutes
  • Schedule Next QBR button to create follow-up meeting
  • Activity/Agentforce tab toggle in right rail
  • Share Minutes, Create Tasks, and Schedule Next QBR action buttons in right rail

Functionality

  • QBR Record & Account Data Loading
    • Loads QBR_Minutes__c record with fields: Partner_Account__c lookup, QBR_Date__c, Next_QBR_Date__c, Notes_Body__c (rich text), and related Partner Account metrics
    • Displays QBR header: Partner account name, meeting date, next scheduled QBR date from linked Account record
  • Partner Scorecard Metrics Display
    • Auto-populates from Partner_Scorecard__c roll-up fields: Health_Score__c, Engagement_Score__c, Growth_Score__c; displays latest Training Completion %, Revenue Attainment %, Certification Completion %
    • Renders color-coded health badges and progress bars for visual assessment
  • Action Items & Meeting Attendees Management
    • Loads QBR_Action_Item__c child records with Action_Item__c description, Owner__c (user/contact), Due_Date__c, Status__c (Not Started, In Progress, Completed); enables checkbox completion status
    • Fetches QBR_Attendee__c junction records linking attendee Contacts with Title__c and Attendee_Email__c for tracking participants
  • Meeting Notes & AI Recommendations
    • Renders QBR_Minutes__c.Notes_Body__c rich text field with collapsible sections for Opening Remarks, Revenue Discussion, Training & Enablement, MDF Utilization, Deal Registration & Pipeline, Next Steps
    • Agentforce NBA analyzes Partner_Scorecard__c metrics trends, certification gaps, and revenue forecast to return strategic recommendations and growth opportunities
  • Document Generation & Distribution
    • Export PDF button generates downloadable document with all QBR content using Salesforce Documents API, includes Partner Scorecard, Action Items table, Meeting Notes, Attendees list
    • Email to Attendees button sends meeting summary email to all QBR_Attendee__c.Attendee_Email__c addresses via Salesforce email service with QBR document attachment
    • Schedule Next QBR button creates new QBR_Minutes__c record with Next_QBR_Date__c preset; Create Tasks button generates Task records for each QBR_Action_Item__c with owner assignment and due date

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • QBR_Minutes__c: Partner_Account__c, QBR_Date__c, Next_QBR_Date__c, Health_Score__c, Engagement_Score__c
  • QBR_Action_Item__c: QBR_Minutes__c, Action_Item__c, Owner__c, Due_Date__c, Status__c
  • QBR_Attendee__c: QBR_Minutes__c, Attendee_Name__c, Attendee_Email__c, Title__c

Validation & Business Logic

  • QBR date must be within 90 days of previous QBR
  • Health score calculated from multiple engagement metrics
  • All action items require owner assignment before QBR closure
  • Meeting minutes required before QBR can be marked complete
