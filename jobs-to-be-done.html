What is a Job to Be Done?

A JTBD describes what a person is trying to accomplish, stated as progress: "[Action] so I can [outcome] without [friction]." Jobs are stable over time — the portal, the screens, and the features exist to fulfil them. Each JTBD here includes the screen(s) that primarily support it.

External jobs belong to Partner-side users (Jordan, Steve, Sam). Internal jobs belong to Equinix-side users operating in Salesforce Lightning (Marcus, Alex, AE, Priya, Rachel).
