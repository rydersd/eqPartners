Design Notes
PAM home vs PSM home

The PSM's home (psm-portfolio.html) is a relationship grid — health scores, engagement, QBR dates, co-sell pipeline. Alex's home is a governance console — approval queues, tier compliance, budget burn, SLA countdowns.

Both users manage the same partner set; they never need to be on the same screen at the same time. The split is intentional.

How PAM roles work at other companies

Cisco: "Partner Account Manager" — one PAM per strategic partner, owns sales quota AND financial relationship. At Cisco's scale, PAM is a field role with revenue targets.

Microsoft: "Partner Development Manager (PDM)" — split between field and HQ teams. PDM handles commercial/go-to-market; a separate Partner Business Executive handles compliance and tier.

AWS: "Partner Development Manager" — more enablement-weighted than financial. Program compliance sits with a separate Partner Operations team. AWS PDMs rarely touch MDF directly.

Palo Alto Networks: "Channel Account Manager" — quota-carrying, closer to a sales role. Financial program governance is centralized in Channel Ops rather than per-partner.

At mid-market vendors: The financial and enablement functions are often one role (no PAM/PSM split). The split we've modeled — PAM for finance/governance, PSM for relationship/enablement — reflects larger program maturity typical of Equinix, Cisco, and Dell Technologies.

Sources: public job postings on LinkedIn (Cisco, Microsoft, AWS, Palo Alto, Equinix PAM/PDM roles, Feb 2026). Analyst framing of PAM/PDM split: Forrester "Channel Program Management" — subscription required, see Further Reading.
