SLA & Window Definitions

Validation (Automated) < 2 hours from submission
PSM Review 2 business days from submission
Protection Windows (from APPROVAL date) New Logo: 90 days | Expansion: 60 days | Renewal: 45 days
Expiry Alerts 30 days, 14 days, 7 days before end date (to Rep & PSM)
Commission Payout Net 30 from close date (subject to qualification)
Deal Desk Review (if flagged) Within 24 hours of close event

Duplicate Detection

PCP runs automated duplicate rule on Customer_Account__c + IBX__c + Product_Family__c within any 90-day lookback window. If a match is found, the new deal is flagged and routed to Marcus (PSM) for investigation. Marcus resolves by either approving the new deal (if legitimate cross-sell) or rejecting as true duplicate. Only one deal per customer-IBX-product combo can hold active protection at a time.

Protection Window Mechanics

Protection window starts on PSM approval date (Step 4), not on submission date (Step 1). This ensures the rep has time to gather customer quote and PSM has time to vet legitimacy before the 90/60/45-day countdown begins. Deal window is visible in both rep-pipeline.html and partner admin portal. If deal expires without closure, protection ends automatically and rep cannot claim commission.

Extension Requests

Rep can request a single extension (up to 30 days max) via portal before window expires. Request routes to PSM for approval based on documented customer engagement or contract term extension. Extension requires PSM signature and reason code. No automatic re-approval; PSM must re-review legitimacy each extension round.

Commission Qualification

Priya Nair (Deal Desk) qualifies commission at close event. Checks: (1) close date within protection window? (2) customer account matches registered partner account? (3) no conflicting claim from another rep? If all pass, Commission__c created at partner's tier rate and stages for Net 30 payout. If dispute, routes to psm-deal-dispute.html for arbitration.

Common Failure Points

Incomplete Form (40%) Rep submits without customer quote or missing TCV — validation rejects, returns to rep with error list.
Duplicate Submission (25%) Same customer + IBX + product detected in 90-day window. Flagged for PSM investigation; rep must provide differentiation (e.g., new product upsell) or deal rejected.
TCV Underestimate (15%) PSM flags unusually low pricing vs. market rate. Requests justification from rep. May reject if customer clearly overstates discount or rep lack documentation.
Expired Protection Window (12%) Deal closes after window expires. Commission disqualified. Rep receives no payout, even if customer truly acquired during protected period (records prove legitimacy post-facto).
Disputed Customer Claim (8%) Another rep or direct account claim detected at close. Routes to psm-deal-dispute.html for arbitration. Commission held pending resolution.

Design Implications

  • Clear Protection Window Visibility: Countdown timer must be prominent in rep dashboard and partner admin portal to reduce "surprise expired" events.
  • Duplicate Detection UX: When system detects duplicate, must show rep the prior deal and let them choose: approve new deal with differentiation note, or cancel. Avoid silent rejections.
  • PSM Workload Scaling: Deal queue must surface urgent items (approaching expiry, customer escalations, disputes). Bulk actions for batch approval of low-risk deals reduce review time.
