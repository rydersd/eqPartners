Master Partner Agreement · MPA-2025-v3.1
Term
Initial 12-month term, auto-renews annually. Either party may terminate with 60 days' written notice.
Intellectual Property
Each party retains ownership of its own IP. Partner may not rebrand or white-label Equinix services without prior written consent.
Confidentiality
Partner agrees to keep Equinix pricing, customer data, and program terms confidential. Survives termination by 3 years.
Liability
Equinix's aggregate liability limited to fees paid in the preceding 3 months.
Termination for Cause
Either party may terminate for material breach with 30-day cure period. Immediate termination for fraud or willful misconduct.
Governing Law
State of California. Venue: Santa Clara County.
