Design Specs

Summary

Dashboard for PSM to manage MDF allocations across partners, review and approve claims with SLA monitoring, and track marketing spend effectiveness by activity type.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Centralizes MDF allocation management across all partner tiers with budget tracking and visibility
  • Streamlines MDF claim approval workflow with 30-day SLA monitoring and bulk action support
  • Tracks marketing ROI performance by activity type to optimize future spending allocation
  • Enables budget rebalancing and tier-based fair distribution with remaining balance monitoring
  • Provides spend analytics and claim history for finance reconciliation and program ROI justification

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • KPI cards: Total Allocated, Spent
  • Fund allocation table
  • Claims queue: Partner, activity type
  • MDF activity type horizontal bar chart: Events, Digital
  • ROI tracker: approved claims with leads, pipeline

Interactive Elements

  • New MDF Allocation and Export Report buttons for workflow initiation
  • Filter dropdowns for Tier, Region
  • Approve/Reject buttons on pending claims with SLA countdown indicators
  • Adjust button per allocation to modify partner budget amounts
  • Pagination controls showing "Showing X of Y" records with Previous/Next navig...

Functionality

  • Allocation & Balance Tracking
    • Query MDF_Allocation__c records filtered by Partner Account and Fiscal_Year__c to display allocation details
    • Calculate Spent balance by summing Approved_Amount__c from MDF_Claim__c records with Status = Approved
    • Calculate Remaining balance as (Allocated_Amount__c - Spent_Amount__c) and format as currency and percentage
  • Claim Validation & Routing
    • Fetch pending MDF_Claim__c records and validate claimed amount does not exceed allocation Remaining__c balance
    • Route claim approvals based on partner tier (Gold: pre/post-execution split 50/50, Silver/Bronze: post-only 100%)
    • Validate proof of execution attached when Claimed_Amount__c > $5K threshold
  • SLA Management & Escalation
    • Auto-calculate SLA expiry date as 30 days from Claim_Submission_Date__c and display countdown indicator
    • Trigger escalation alert workflow when SLA < 5 days remaining with email notification to approver
    • Auto-archive claims if SLA exceeded 30 days without approval (set Status to Expired)
  • ROI Metrics & Performance Tracking
    • Generate ROI metrics from approved claims: sum Leads_Sourced__c, aggregate Pipeline_Value__c created from activity
    • Calculate conversion rate from leads to pipeline using Deal_Registration__c linked records
    • Display cost per lead metric as (Claimed_Amount__c / Leads_Sourced__c) and compare across activity types
  • Reporting & Approval Workflow
    • Submit MDF requests for approval with SLA tracking and configurable approval routing by tier
    • Approve/Reject claims with optional comment feedback routed back to partner
    • Auto-update Spent_Amount__c on MDF_Allocation__c when claim approved and maintain real-time balance accuracy

Technical Details

Validation & Business Logic

  • MDF_Allocation__c: Partner_Account__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Allocated_Amount__c, Spent_Amount__c, Rema...
  • MDF_Claim__c: Allocation__c, Activity_Type__c, Claimed_Amount__c, Claim_Status__c, Submission_Dat...
  • MDF_Activity_Type__mdt: Name, Description__c, Category__c, Display_Order__c
  • Claim amount cannot exceed remaining allocation balance
  • Proof of execution required for amounts > $5K; auto-optional for ≤ $5K
  • Claims must be submitted within 90 days of activity completion (Activity_Date__c + 90 days)
  • Gold partners: 50% pre-approval, 50% post-execution; Silver/Bronze: 100% post-execution
  • Fiscal year allocation expires if not claimed by March 31 (auto-archive)
