Communication Guidelines

All messages sent here are logged to the partner's SFDC record and are visible to Channel Ops.

Merge fields pull live data: {{mdf_remaining}}, {{partner_first_name}}, etc.

Do not send tier demotion notices without Channel Ops approval. Use the Tier Review Notice template which routes through the correct workflow.

Screen Notes

Screen

psm-partner-comms.html — Partner communications hub for PSM Marcus Chen. Compose view is active by default, pre-populated with a draft MDF reminder targeted at Gold-tier partners (CloudBridge, InnovateTech, Meridian).

Job to Be Done

"When I need to reach one or more partners, I want to send targeted, logged communications without leaving the portal, so that partners get relevant information fast and every message is traceable in the partner record."

→ See full workflow: PSM Job 5: Partner Communications

Business Logic

  • Audience segment builder allows broadcast (all of tier, health filter, MDF filter) or individual. A partner can be removed from a broadcast via the chip remove button.
  • All sent messages are logged to each partner's SFDC record. Open tracking is native via SFDC email integration.
  • Merge fields pull live data from SFDC at send time, not at draft time.
  • Tier demotion notices require Channel Ops approval before send. The template routes correctly; manual drafts do not.
  • Partners who opt out of broadcasts still receive individual PSM messages (transactional).

Key Components

  • Audience segment builder: 5 filter options (tier × 3, health at-risk, MDF under-utilized) plus individual dropdown. Selected audiences show partner names + contact count inline.
  • Recipient chips: Preview of who will receive the message. Individual chips are removable.
  • Template selector: In toolbar dropdown — applies template body + subject on select. 6 pre-built templates.
  • Merge fields: Variables shown with {{handlebars}} syntax. Merge field button in toolbar opens a picker.
  • Compose body: Pre-populated with MDF reminder draft targeting Gold partners. Realistic placeholder copy.
  • Sent history: 5 most recent messages with open tracking status. TerraCloud's unopened message flagged in amber.
  • Tabs: Compose / Sent / Templates / Scheduled. Only Compose tab is wired; others are navigation stubs.

Data Story Continuity
