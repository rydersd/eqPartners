Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register a new deal? ▼
Go to Deals & Commission page and click "Register New Deal". Fill in customer details, product/service, estimated amount, and expected close date. Submit for approval. Equinix PSM will review within 2-3 business days. Once approved, track deal progress through pipeline stages.
What are the commission rates for each tier? ▼
Commission rates are: Bronze 8%, Silver 12%, Gold 15%. Rates apply to Closed Won deals. Commission is automatically calculated and paid monthly on the 15th following deal closure. Download the Commission Structure Guide for complete details including incentive programs.
How long are certifications valid? ▼
Most Equinix partner certifications are valid for 2 years from issue date. Some technical certifications may have different validity periods. Check the Certifications page for expiration dates on your active certs. You'll receive renewal reminders 90 days before expiration.
What training courses are required for Silver tier? ▼
Silver tier requires 8 courses: 4 business track and 4 technical track. Required courses are: Our Mission/Vision, Colocation Overview, Interconnection Overview, Hybrid Multicloud, plus 4 additional courses from the catalog. See My Training page for complete list and progress tracking.

Design Specs

Summary

Comprehensive resource library providing partners with quick access to sales tools, technical documentation, guides, FAQs, and support contacts. Organized by category with search and filtering to help partners find relevant materials quickly and improve their sales effectiveness.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Access organized resource library across categories
  • Download technical specs and product information
  • Find answers to frequently asked questions
  • Quick access to most recently used resources
  • Contact support team for additional assistance
  • Discover program documentation and guidelines

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Page title with resource library overview
  • Tabbed sections for resource categories: Sales Tools, Technical Specs, Program Guides, FAQs
  • Resource cards displaying: thumbnail/icon, title, description, type badge, download link
  • Recently Used Resources section showing previous downloads
  • AI-recommended resources based on user history
  • Frequently Asked Questions expandable list with question-answer pairs
  • Support contact information card with team availability
  • Actions section with Search and Contact Support buttons

Interactive Elements

  • Tab switching between resource categories without page reload
  • Resource card click opens preview or triggers download
  • Search input filters resources and FAQs by keyword
  • Filter dropdown by topic or product area
  • FAQ question click toggles answer visibility
  • Contact Support button opens email or chat widget
  • Recently Used Resources showing links to frequently accessed items

Functionality

  • Resource Discovery & Filtering
    • Tab switching filters visible resource cards based on category without page reload
    • Search operation queries resource metadata (title, description, tags) using full-text indexes
    • Resource filtering by product maintains category tab state in localStorage
  • Content Access & Downloads
    • Resource download links route to file storage systems or external documentation platforms
    • Recently used resources populated from user activity logs and audit trails
    • Access controls validate user permissions before exposing resource links
  • Search & Recommendations
    • FAQ search filters question list by text matching using client-side or server-side search
    • AI recommendations generated based on user's role and recently accessed resource affinity patterns
  • Support & Engagement
    • Contact Support button routes to help desk system or initiates chat session with support team

Technical Details

Validation & Business Logic

  • Resources stored in Content Management System with category and tag metadata
  • Recently used resources tracked from download audit logs
  • Search indexes resource titles and descriptions for fast lookup
  • AI recommendations calculate based on user activity and role-based content affinity
