Notes & Spec

EUC Notification Center

Notifications are generated by three primary sources:

  1. Equinix automated systems — maintenance, billing, SLA breach alerts
  2. Partner activity — Stark Industries case responses, order status changes
  3. PSM activity — Marcus Chen escalations and priority updates

Unread Count Badge: The header badge displays the count of notifications with status=unread. "Mark all read" clears the badge and updates all visible notifications to read status.

Notification Preferences: Toggles control email/SMS delivery channels. In-portal notifications are always shown regardless of preference settings.

Critical Alerts: Alert and SLA breach notifications trigger email delivery to maria.hill@shield.gov regardless of SMS or digest preferences.

Color Coding: Left border indicates notification type — amber for maintenance/contract, red for SLA breaches, blue for cases/system, grey for standard order/billing updates.
