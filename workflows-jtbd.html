Jobs to Be Done

How each persona uses the Partner Connect Portal — framed as the jobs they're trying to accomplish.

Why Jobs to Be Done?

Each screen in this portal exists to help a specific person accomplish a specific job. Jobs to Be Done (JTBD) framing keeps design decisions grounded in real goals rather than features. Each job has the structure: "When [situation] → I want to [motivation] → so I can [outcome]."

The links below connect each job to the wireframe screens that implement it. Use this page as a navigation layer between persona intent and portal execution.

AR

Alex Rivera

Partner Account Manager (PAM) • Equinix Channel Programs
PAM

Alex owns the business relationship between Equinix and its partner ecosystem. The job is the financial and compliance gatekeeper: reviewing applications, managing MDF funds, enforcing tier rules, and reporting program health to leadership. Every decision Alex makes affects which partners are in the program and what resources they receive.

1
Application Review — Evaluate and decision partner applications within SLA
When a new partner application arrives in the queue, I want to assess the company's fit, financial standing, and tier recommendation quickly, so I can make a tier decision within the 30-day SLA and avoid letting applications stall.

What Alex needs to do this job

  • See all open applications sorted by days-in-queue with SLA status at a glance
  • Spot compliance gaps (missing W-9, insurance lapse) without opening each record
  • Understand the tier recommendation (Bronze/Silver/Gold) with supporting rationale
  • Identify risk flags: resubmissions, international applications requiring extra review, overdue items
  • Approve, deny, or request more information in a single click from the queue

Screens

→ pam-application-queue → pam-partner-financial (per-partner detail)
2
Tier Management — Monitor tier health and intervene before partners miss thresholds
When managing the partner program at-scale, I want a program-wide view of which partners are at risk of demotion and which are candidates for upgrade, so I can intervene early enough to matter — not after the tier review date has passed.

What Alex needs to do this job

  • See all 42 partners' tier status, revenue gaps, and days-to-review in one view
  • Drill into any partner's full financial record: MDF spend, rebates, revenue trajectory
  • Identify partners who are on a demotion path so Marcus (PSM) can be looped in early
  • Surface upgrade candidates who are close to the next tier threshold
  • See insurance and certification compliance status per partner to catch renewal gaps

Screens

→ pam-tier-dashboard → pam-partner-financial
3
MDF Approval — Review and approve MDF fund requests efficiently
When a partner submits an MDF fund request, I want to validate the business case, check tier budget availability, and approve or deny within the SLA, so partners can execute their campaigns on time and program budget is allocated to high-ROI activities.

What Alex needs to do this job

  • See all pending MDF requests in one queue with SLA countdown and tier budget utilization
  • Understand the requested activity type and whether it qualifies under program rules
  • Check the partner's remaining tier budget before approving — prevent overruns
  • Spot budget exhaustion warnings (Bronze tier at 98% in the current period)
  • Distinguish pre-approval from post-approval claim requests in the queue

Screens

→ pam-mdf-queue
4
Claim Reimbursement — Verify proof of spend and process MDF reimbursements
When a partner submits proof-of-spend documentation for a completed MDF campaign, I want to verify that the activity matches the approved scope, the claim is within budget, and the documentation is complete, so I can process the reimbursement quickly and partners trust the program pays out on time.

What Alex needs to do this job

  • See the claimed amount vs approved amount alongside the proof documents in one view
  • Verify that claimed activity matches the approved scope (e.g., LinkedIn Ads were what was approved)
  • Review ROI evidence: leads generated, pipeline attributed, cost per MQL
  • Run through a compliance checklist: claim ≤ approved, activity within date window, within 90-day claim window
  • View the partner's full MDF history to understand patterns (over-claiming, late submissions)

Screens

→ pam-mdf-claims
5
Program Reporting — Build the quarterly program health report for leadership
When building the quarterly program performance report for Channel leadership, I want to aggregate partner-sourced ARR, MDF ROI, tier distribution, and risk flags into a single executive view, so I can demonstrate the program's value and flag any investments or interventions needed in the next quarter.

What Alex needs to do this job

  • See total partner-sourced ARR, total MDF invested, and blended ROI in one view
  • Break down performance by tier: which tier is generating the most value per dollar invested?
  • Identify the top-performing partners and flag the 6 at churn risk
  • Show ARR trajectory quarter-over-quarter with a Q2 forecast
  • Export the report as a PDF to share with the Channel VP before the leadership review

Screens

→ pam-program-report
MC

Marcus Chen

Partner Success Manager (PSM) • Equinix Channel Sales
PSM

Marcus owns the post-approval partner relationship. Where Alex manages the program's business rules, Marcus manages the day-to-day relationship: co-selling alongside partners, resolving disputes, running QBRs, and making sure each partner in his portfolio of 8 is on track to grow. Marcus is the human face of the Equinix partner program.

1
Portfolio Triage — Start each week knowing exactly which partners need attention
When I start my week, I want a single view of all 8 partners with health scores, pipeline status, and urgent actions surfaced in priority order, so I can spend my first 15 minutes on the right things instead of assembling the picture from scratch.

What Marcus needs to do this job

  • See all 8 partners at a glance with color-coded health (green/yellow/red)
  • Understand the 3 most urgent priority actions for the week without digging into individual records
  • Spot open deal disputes, overdue QBRs, and partners with no pipeline activity
  • Navigate directly from a partner card into the relevant action (dispute view, QBR prep, comms)
  • See which partners are upgrade candidates and which are at risk of demotion

Screens

→ psm-portfolio
2
Deal Dispute Resolution — Assess attribution disputes fairly and close them before they escalate
When a deal dispute surfaces between a partner and Equinix direct sales, I want to review the evidence from both sides, assess the timeline against program rules, and submit a clear recommendation, so I can protect partner trust in the program and close the case before the 21-day escalation deadline.

What Marcus needs to do this job

  • See the full dispute timeline: who logged the deal first, when deal registration happened, what evidence each party submitted
  • Understand the financial stake: deal value, attribution bonus, and downstream MDF ROI impact
  • Review program rules (§4.2 deal registration controls attribution) with the evidence side by side
  • See precedent from similar resolved cases to anchor the recommendation
  • File a recommendation (uphold partner, co-source split, or escalate) with a written rationale

Screens

→ psm-deal-dispute
3
Co-sell Collaboration — Stay actively engaged on partner-registered deals to accelerate close
When a partner registers a deal and needs Equinix's help to win it, I want to co-sell alongside them by coordinating internal resources, tracking next steps on both sides, and keeping the deal moving through each stage, so the partner closes faster and attributes the win to the relationship with Equinix.

What Marcus needs to do this job

  • See the deal stage, close date, and attribution status at the top without scrolling
  • Track separate next actions for Equinix and the partner — both are accountable
  • Log activity and coordinate SA engagement, Deal Desk requests, and Exec Sponsor involvement
  • See all other open deals for the same partner to manage capacity and prioritization
  • Access co-sell resources (battle cards, reference architectures, pricing tools) in context

Screens

→ psm-deal-cosell
4
QBR Preparation — Build a data-driven QBR brief that leads to specific partner commitments
When a partner QBR is approaching, I want to compile performance data, surface risks, and define concrete asks ahead of the call, so I walk in with a narrative — not a spreadsheet — and leave with specific commitments from the partner.

What Marcus needs to do this job

  • See a readiness checklist for all QBR sections — which are complete, which still need data
  • Auto-populate metrics from SFDC: ARR delta, pipeline by stage, MDF utilization vs benchmark
  • Review the training and certification gap so the ask is specific ("enroll second staff by Mar 15")
  • Define asks by type: commitment, feedback, or risk conversation — so the agenda has clear purpose
  • Export a professional QBR deck without needing to work outside the portal

Screens

→ psm-qbr-prep
5
Partner Communications — Send targeted, logged communications to the right partners without leaving the portal
When I need to reach one or more of my 8 partners — a broadcast update, an individual alert, or a deadline reminder, I want to compose, target, and send messages with merge fields from inside the portal, so every communication is logged to the partner record and I'm not managing partner outreach from my personal inbox.

What Marcus needs to do this job

  • Segment recipients by tier, health score, or MDF utilization — not by manually picking contacts
  • Use templates with merge fields so messages are personalized but scalable
  • See open/engagement tracking so he knows which partners aren't reading communications
  • Schedule messages ahead of deadlines (e.g., MDF expiry warning 5 days before cutoff)
  • Access sent history per partner so he can see the full communication trail before a call

Screens

→ psm-partner-comms

Steve Rogers

Sales Rep / Account Executive • CloudBridge Solutions (Partner)
AE

Steve is the partner-side AE at CloudBridge Solutions. His job is to find and close deals that include Equinix infrastructure, and to use the PCP to register those deals, access MDF funds, and engage Equinix support when he needs it. The portal should make Steve faster — not create process overhead.

1
Deal Registration — Protect your deal and get partner-sourced attribution
When I qualify a prospect who needs Equinix infrastructure, I want to register the deal in the portal quickly and get confirmation before Equinix direct finds them, so I have protected attribution and the deal reg bonus when it closes.

Screens

→ psm-deal-cosell (co-sell view)

Note: Steve's deal registration entry point is a separate partner-facing screen (not yet wired). The co-sell view shows what the deal looks like after registration from Marcus's perspective.

2
MDF Campaign — Access and spend MDF funds to generate pipeline
When planning a demand gen campaign with Equinix co-branding, I want to submit an MDF request, get fast approval, run the activity, and submit proof-of-spend without chasing anyone, so the campaign happens on the timeline I planned and I get reimbursed within the pay period.

Screens

→ pm-mdf-balance (Steve checks available budget first) → pm-mdf-request (Steve submits the request) → pm-mdf-comarketing (co-marketing activity planner) → pm-pop-submission (post-event proof of performance) → pam-mdf-queue (Alex approves the request) → pam-mdf-claims (Alex processes the claim)
3
MDF-3 — Dispute a rejected co-marketing claim
When my PAM rejects a POP claim I believe is valid, I want to formally dispute the decision with my supporting evidence and get a second review, so I'm not writing off reimbursement that I'm entitled to, and the program maintains trust that decisions are fair.

Screens

→ pm-pop-submission (original POP submission) → pm-mdf-dispute (Steve disputes the rejection) → pam-mdf-claims (Alex sees the dispute, reviews) → cmm-mdf-oversight (Priya escalation if PAM denies again)

Compliance Maintenance — Keep all certifications, documents, and user accounts current

When applying to the Equinix partner program (or re-tiering), I want to complete the application in one session with clear guidance on what's required at each tier, so the application doesn't stall due to missing documents and CloudBridge gets a decision within the SLA.

AR

Tier Demotion & Appeal Flow

Alex Rivera (PAM) initiates · Jordan Reeves (Partner Admin) responds
PAM + Partner

When a partner misses tier requirements, the PAM needs to initiate demotion with proper governance and give the partner a fair chance to appeal. Both sides have distinct surfaces for this flow.

T1
Tier-1 (PAM) — Initiate partner tier demotion with governance gates
When a partner has missed requirements for a full quarter, I want to initiate the demotion process with a clear requirements scorecard, a two-step action (warn first / demote), and a required confirmation before it takes effect, so the partner has fair warning, I'm protected from contract disputes, and the decision is traceable to PSM consultation and regional approval.

Screens

→ pam-home-dashboard (at-risk partner panel) → pam-tier-demotion (PAM initiates demotion) → pm-tier-appeal (partner sees notice, submits appeal)
T2
Tier-2 (Partner) — Dispute a tier demotion with evidence
When I receive a demotion notice I believe is based on incomplete data, I want to submit a formal appeal with supporting evidence within the 30-day window while retaining my current tier during review, so I don't lose deal protections and discounts based on an attribution error I can prove.

Screens

→ pm-tier-appeal (partner appeal form with 30-day countdown) → pam-tier-demotion (PAM side — receives and reviews appeal)

Jamie Torres

Lead Referral Partner • Meridian Consulting Group
Referral

Jamie submits warm leads to Equinix without reselling. No deal registration, no MDF, no tier system. Jamie's value to Equinix is pure pipeline introduction — and Jamie's value from Equinix is a transparent commission check. The surface is deliberately lightweight.

1
Referral-1 — Submit a warm lead and track it to commission
When a client of mine is a strong fit for Equinix colocation or interconnection, I want to introduce them quickly, see exactly what stage the lead is in, and know when I'll get paid, so I keep referring without worrying that leads disappear or commissions get lost in the process.

Screens

→ onboarding-apply-referral (Jamie's onboarding) → ref-home (post-onboarding home, quick-submit, commission tracker) → ref-submit (stub — full lead submission form) → ref-commissions (stub — full commission history)

PN

Priya Nair

Channel Marketing Manager • Equinix
CMM

Priya sets MDF budgets by tier, designs co-marketing programs, and monitors ROI. She sits above PAMs in the MDF chain — PAMs approve individual requests within the budget Priya allocates. When partners underutilize, Priya investigates and reallocates. When partners over-claim or submit ineligible activities, they land in her oversight queue.

1
CMM-1 — Allocate MDF budgets and monitor program-wide utilization
At the start of each fiscal quarter, I want to set per-tier MDF allocations, track utilization in real-time, and flag underutilizing partners before budget expires, so MDF spend converts to measurable pipeline ROI rather than expiring unspent or being used on low-value activities.

Screens

→ cmm-home-dashboard (Priya's budget + utilization overview) → cmm-budget-allocation (stub — tier-level budget setter) → cmm-campaign-calendar (stub — co-marketing event calendar) → cmm-mdf-oversight (stub — escalated/flagged claims queue) → pam-mdf-queue (PAM approves within Priya's budget envelope) → pm-mdf-dispute (partner escalates rejected claim to Priya)

RK

Rachel Kim

Deal Desk Analyst • Equinix (Internal)
Deal Desk

Rachel processes special pricing exceptions that PAMs escalate when a deal requires discounts above standard caps, non-standard contract terms, compensation overrides, or deal timeline extensions. She sits between the PAM layer and Finance/VP approval, operating under strict time-bound SLAs on urgent deals.

1
DD-1 — Review and resolve partner deal exception requests within SLA
When a PAM escalates a deal that needs a pricing exception, non-standard terms, or discount over cap, I want to see the PAM's justification, the deal value, and the competitive context in one place and make a fast, traceable decision, so competitive deals don't stall waiting for internal approvals and every decision has an audit trail.

Screens

→ pam-home-dashboard (PAM submits exception from deal view) → dealdesk-queue (Rachel's prioritized exception review queue) → dealdesk-approved (stub — approved request archive) → dealdesk-denied (stub — denied request archive) → dealdesk-analytics (stub — approval rates, cycle time) → pm-deal-registration (partner sees updated deal terms if approved)
Design Notes
What this page is

This is a design reference document — not a portal screen. It maps the Jobs to Be Done for each persona and links them to the wireframe screens that implement each job.

JTBD ID stability

Each job has an anchor ID (e.g. #pam-jtbd-1, #psm-jtbd-3). These IDs are referenced from personas.html and ia-spine-v0.1.html. Do not renumber without updating those links.
