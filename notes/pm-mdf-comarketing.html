Salesforce Objects & Fields
MDF_Allocation__c: MDF ID, Account ID (lookup), Annual_Budget__c, Spent__c, Remaining__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Expiration_Date__c
MDF_Claim__c: Claim ID, MDF_Allocation__c (lookup), Activity_Type__c, Amount_Requested__c, Amount_Approved__c, Status__c (Pending/Approved/Paid/Rejected), Proof_of_Execution__c (File), Submission_Date__c, Approval_Date__c, Payment_Date__c
MDF_Activity_Type__mdt: Activity Type ID, Name, Max_Claim_Amount__c, Requires_Pre_Approval__c, Documentation_Required__c
Validation Rules & Edge Cases
  • Claim amount cannot exceed remaining balance (Remaining__c > Amount_Requested__c)
  • Proof of execution required for all claims (file attachment mandatory)
  • Claims must be submitted within 90 days of activity completion
  • Gold: 50% pre-approval available; Silver: post-execution only; Bronze: 25% pre-approval
  • Annual allocation resets Jan 1 each year; unused funds do NOT carry over
  • Multiple claims for same activity not allowed (prevent double dipping)
Error States
  • "Allocation depleted — contact your PSM for additional MDF funds"
  • "File upload failed — max 10MB per attachment, PDF/Word/Excel only"
  • "Claim submitted outside 90-day window — contact support"
  • "Claim amount exceeds activity max ($X allowed for [Activity Type])"
Accessibility & User Flow
  1. PM reviews MDF balance card (annual allocation, spent, remaining)
  2. PM browses eligible activities list
  3. PM executes marketing activity (event, campaign, etc.)
  4. PM submits claim with proof of execution (PDF/receipt)
  5. PM tracks claim status in history table
  6. Claim approved; PM receives reimbursement
Accessibility: MDF balance displayed in large text; activity cards have descriptive labels; tables sortable by date/status; file upload has drag-drop + button options; all status badges use color + text
Related Automations
Flows: MDF_Claim_Submission_Flow (sends confirmation email), MDF_Approval_Workflow (routes to PSM), MDF_Payment_Processing_Flow (monthly on 25th)
Apex Triggers: MDF_Balance_UpdateTrigger (on Claim insert), MDF_Expiration_CheckTrigger (Jan 1 each year)
Batch Jobs: Annual_MDF_Reset_Batch (Jan 1)

Design Notes

Summary

The MDF & Co-Marketing dashboard enables Equinix to manage fund allocations, approve partner marketing campaigns, track spend against budget, and measure ROI across the partner channel.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Allocate annual MDF and co-marketing funds to partners
  • Review and approve partner marketing campaign requests
  • Track fund expenditures and remaining budgets in real time
  • Monitor ROI of marketing investments and campaign performance
  • Ensure compliance with partner agreement terms and fund policies

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • MDF fund allocation dashboard by partner tier and region
  • Budget utilization summary cards (allocated, spent, remaining, % utilization)
  • Partner co-marketing request queue with approval status
  • Active campaigns table with spend tracking and ROI metrics
  • Fund lifecycle timeline showing allocations, resets, and expiry
  • Approval workflow status view by request and date

Interactive Elements

  • Click partner row to drill down to fund allocation details
  • Approve/reject campaign requests with comment field and audit trail
  • View campaign ROI metrics, lead attribution, and deal influence
  • Adjust budget allocations with approval required and notification
  • Download fund utilization reports by partner and period
  • Export campaign performance data for external stakeholder review

Functionality

  • Fund Management & Calculation
    • Real-time fund balance calculation: Allocated_Amount__c - sum(campaign spends) with live balance display
    • Quarterly fund reset and rollover automation per fiscal calendar and Partner_Agreement__c terms
  • Validation & Spend Control
    • Spend validation rule: Campaign_Budget__c ≤ available_fund_balance before approval to prevent overspend
    • Campaign approval workflow routing to PSM/approver with auto-expiration at 30 days for uncompleted reviews
  • ROI Tracking & Audit
    • ROI calculation: (attributed_leads + influenced_deals) / campaign_spend with lead-to-deal conversion modeling
    • Audit trail logging for all allocation changes, approvals, and spend transactions for compliance

Technical Details

Key Objects

  • MDF_Fund__c: Partner_Account__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Allocated_Amount__c, Status__c, Approval_Date__c
  • Marketing_Campaign__c: Fund__c, Campaign_Name__c, Budget_Amount__c, Status__c, ROI_Pct__c

Validation & Business Logic

  • Fund allocations limited to annual partner agreement amounts
  • Campaign spend cannot exceed allocated fund balance
  • All campaigns require PSM or higher approval
  • Requests auto-expire after 30 days without approval decision
