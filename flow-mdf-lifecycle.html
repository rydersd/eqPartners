MDF Lifecycle Process Flow

Portal: Equinix Partner Connect (PCP) Version: 1.0 Updated: 2026-02-27

Key Business Rules

  • MDF available to Gold (limited) and Platinum tier partners only
  • Requests must be submitted BEFORE activity start date — no retroactive approvals
  • Execution window: 90 days from approval date
  • Claims must be submitted within 30 days of activity end
  • Maximum reimbursement: 50% of approved budget (matching requirement)
  • Proof required: receipts + attendance/outcome evidence

Dispute Path (If Claim Rejected at Step 6)

Jordan submits dispute via pm-mdf-dispute.html → Marcus (PSM) reviews and investigates scope mismatch → Marcus mediates and may request additional proof or negotiate revised reimbursement → Resolution sent back to Alex (PAM) for final decision. If escalation needed, reviewed by Equinix Program Director.
End-to-End Timeline: Request → Approval (5 biz days) → Execution (up to 90 days) → Claim (within 30 days of activity) → Review (10 biz days) → Reimbursement (5–10 biz days). Total: ~4–5 months typical.
