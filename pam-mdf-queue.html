PAM Portal · Alex Rivera · MDF Request Queue

Job to Be Done

"When a partner submits an MDF request, I want to validate the business case against the program budget and tier allocation — so I can approve or deny with a clear audit trail within the 10-business-day SLA."

Screen Purpose

Alex's daily queue for reviewing and approving/denying inbound MDF fund requests. Partners submit requests through the partner portal; they land here for PAM review. The program budget summary cards at top prevent over-allocation — especially for Bronze tier, which shows near-exhaustion.

Key Design Decisions

  • Budget summary by tier at top — prevents approval errors. If Bronze budget is 98% committed, Alex should not approve new Bronze requests without escalation
  • Pre-approval vs. post-execution shown per request — Gold partners can request pre-approval (money before execution); Silver/Bronze are post-execution only. This changes the approval logic and when Alex validates proof
  • Partner balance shown inline — Alex should never approve a request that exceeds the partner's remaining allocation; showing balance in the row catches this instantly
  • SLA countdown chips — color-coded (red <3 days, yellow 3–7, green 7+) so the queue naturally sorts itself visually
  • Approve and Deny as separate action buttons — not a dropdown, because the wrong selection has real financial consequences and should require a deliberate tap

Business Logic

  • SLA: 10 business days from partner submission date
  • Approval prerequisite: requested amount ≤ partner's remaining MDF balance
  • Bronze tier: post-execution only (no pre-approval); amounts over $5K require proof of execution attachment
  • Approval triggers notification to partner portal; Approved_Date__c and Approver__c fields logged
  • Denial requires comment before submission (shown in modal, not in queue view)
