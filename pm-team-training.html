Salesforce Objects & Fields
Contact: Contact ID, Name, Email, Phone, AccountContactRelation (for PM visibility)
Partner_Training__c: Training ID, Contact ID (lookup), Course__c (lookup), Status__c (Completed/In Progress/Not Started), Completion_Date__c, Enrollment_Date__c
Course__c: Course ID, Name, Type (Business/Technical/Elective), Duration_Hours__c, Max_Attendees__c, Status__c
Account (Partner Account): Account ID, Name, Partner_Tier__c, Team_Completion_Rate__c (rollup), Last_Training_Sync__c
Validation Rules & Edge Cases
  • PM can only see contacts linked to their account via AccountContactRelation
  • Send Reminder respects 24-hour cooldown per recipient (track on Contact.Last_Reminder_Date__c)
  • Bulk remind max 20 recipients at once (prevent email throttling)
  • Course completion data read-only — sourced from SumTotal LMS sync (daily 2 AM UTC)
  • Team completion % = Sum(individual %)/Team Member Count
  • Tier requirements checked nightly via batch job Tier_Validation_Batch__c
Error States
  • "No team members found — invite team to portal first"
  • "Training data sync delayed — showing data as of [last sync timestamp]"
  • "Unable to send reminders — contact email for [name] is invalid"
  • "Course enrollment failed — max capacity reached (contact admin)"
  • "LMS sync in progress — data will refresh in ~5 minutes"
Accessibility Notes
  • Tables use proper th/td semantics for screen readers
  • Expandable rows have aria-expanded attribute
  • Status badges use color + text (never color alone)
  • Progress bars have aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax
  • Tab navigation uses role="tablist" and aria-selected
  • Buttons have title attributes for hover tooltips
User Flow (Numbered Steps)
  1. PM navigates to Team Training page
  2. PM sees org-level progress (95%) and tier status (Gold, 95% of requirements)
  3. PM switches to "By Team Member" tab (default)
  4. PM sorts by completion % (lowest first) to identify gaps
  5. PM identifies 2 at-risk members (Peter Parker 50%, Wanda Maximoff 58%)
  6. PM clicks row to expand and see individual course status
  7. PM selects members and sends bulk reminder or clicks "Send to All Behind"
  8. PM switches to "By Course" tab to find systemic gaps
  9. PM exports team report (CSV/PDF) for internal team meeting
  10. PM checks back in 1 week to verify completion

Design Specs

Summary

Stark Industries (Gold tier, Pepper Potts as logged-in user) Team Training dashboard shows 95% org completion rate with 9 team members. Gold tier requirements: 11 of 12 met (1 course gap: Advanced Security for Pepper Potts). CRITICAL ALERT: Sarah Connor's Security Fundamentals cert EXPIRED Jan 15, 2026 — must recertify immediately. Peter Parker (20%, new hire Jan 30) and Natasha Romanoff (45%) behind on certifications. Top performer: Tony Stark 100% complete.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Monitor team-wide training compliance rate
  • Track individual member progress by business and technical tracks
  • Identify members behind on requirements
  • Send reminders individually or in bulk to members needing action
  • View course-level completion metrics across entire team
  • Export team training report for management or internal meetings
  • Recognize top performers via leaderboard

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Page header with overall org completion rate and progress bar
  • Tier Progress card showing Current Tier and progress toward next tier
  • Tier Requirements summary banner highlighting compliance criteria
  • Team member table with individual progress, completion %, and status
  • Course view tab with enrollment counts and completion rates per course
  • Track view tab showing Business/Technical/Elective track completion status
  • Training Leaderboard section with top 10 performers

Interactive Elements

  • Tab buttons to switch between By Team Member and By Course views
  • Expandable table rows to drill into course-level breakdown per member
  • Status badges indicating On Track, At Risk, or Behind
  • Bulk action buttons: Send Reminder to Selected Members, Send to All Behind
  • Sort and filter controls (name, progress %, status)
  • Export to CSV button for report generation
  • Leaderboard rank badges for top performers

Functionality

  • Team Member Querying & Filtering
    • Query CourseAssignment__c records for user's account team members via AccountContactRelation
    • Retrieve course completion data from SumTotal LMS integration synced daily at 2 AM UTC
    • Filter display by account, status, or responsibility area for targeted visibility
  • Progress Calculation & Risk Assessment
    • Calculate team completion % as (sum of individual completion %) / team member count
    • Identify at-risk members where completion % < tier requirement threshold with visual flagging
    • Auto-update Tier Progress based on member enrollment in required learning paths
  • Leaderboard & Ranking
    • Rank leaderboard by completion % DESC and Recent_Course_Completion_Date__c for ranking
    • Generate top 10 performers list with rank badges and completion status
    • Update leaderboard daily from synced LMS data for current standings
  • Notifications & Bulk Operations
    • Enforce Send Reminder 24-hour cooldown via Contact.Last_Reminder_Date__c to prevent spam
    • Limit bulk remind operations to 20 recipients max (email throttle protection)
    • Enable Export to CSV for reporting and offline analysis of team training data

Technical Details

  • Send Reminder respects 24-hour cooldown per recipient (track on Contact.Last_Reminder_Date__c)
  • Bulk remind max 20 recipients at once (prevent email throttling)
  • Course completion data read-only sourced from SumTotal LMS sync (daily 2 AM UTC)
