Design Specs

Summary

Pipeline dashboard providing kanban-style deal visualization with progress tracking, filtering, and comprehensive deal management. Partners monitor deal status across approval workflow stages, forecast revenue, and manage multiple opportunities from a single interface.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Track deal progression through approval workflow stages
  • Monitor total pipeline value and revenue forecast across time periods
  • Understand deal protection periods and approval requirements
  • Manage multiple deals across different stages simultaneously
  • View recently won deals and celebrate wins
  • Forecast quarterly and annual revenue based on deal pipeline
  • Alert on deals nearing expiration of protection periods

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Page title with total pipeline value and unregistered opportunities alert
  • Kanban board displaying deal pipeline stages (Prospect, Registered, Under Review, Won)
  • Deal cards within each column showing: deal name, customer, deal value
  • Filter bar with stage, status, and product category filters
  • Active opportunities table with columns: Deal Name, Customer, Amount, Stage, Days in Stage, Next Action
  • Won deals summary section with recent closures and total closed amount

Interactive Elements

  • Deal cards are clickable to open detail view or edit page
  • Hover on cards shows additional options (edit, move, delete)
  • Stage filter dropdown to show only deals in selected stage
  • Status filter options (Active, Stalled, At Risk)
  • Product category filter to focus on specific product lines
  • Sort controls in table for revenue, date, or stage
  • Won deals section shows recent closures with dates and amounts

Functionality

  • Pipeline Visualization & Stage Management
    • Kanban card positions reflect Opportunity stage field (Prospect, Qualified, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed/Won)
    • Deal stage updates via drag-and-drop or form submission sync immediately to Opportunity record in Salesforce
    • Deal values aggregate by stage to calculate stage totals displayed in column headers
  • Filtering & Display Updates
    • Filtering updates kanban display and table rows without page reload using client-side visibility toggles or SOQL queries
    • Sort controls in table reorder deals by revenue, date, or stage using dynamic sorting logic
  • Alert & Notification Logic
    • Expiration alert triggered when deal protection period is within 30 days of end date via scheduled Flow or Apex job
    • Alert banner displays conditionally based on deal count with expiring protection periods
  • Revenue & Reporting
    • Revenue forecast calculated from pipeline deals using Amount field with win probability factor
    • Won deals section queries closed deals from current fiscal period using SOQL
    • Total value in page header sums all active opportunity amounts
  • Navigation & Details
    • Click deal to navigate to deal detail page with full opportunity context and related records

Technical Details

Validation & Business Logic

  • Opportunity records queried and grouped by StageName field
  • Deal protection expiration calculated from Protection_End_Date__c field
  • Revenue totals aggregated using Amount field, filtered by RecordTypeId
  • Stage filtering validates user has access to view deals in selected stage
