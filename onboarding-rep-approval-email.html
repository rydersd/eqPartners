PCP-REP-APPROVE Transactional · Experience Cloud auto-send on admin approval action · Trigger: PartnerUserRequest__c.Status → "Approved"

About This Email

PCP-REP-APPROVE is the activation email sent to an individual user after a Portal Admin approves their join request. It is distinct from PCP-APPROVE, which is sent to the org admin after Equinix approves a new partner organization's application.

Trigger

Sent automatically when PartnerUserRequest__c.Status is updated to "Approved" by a user with the Portal Admin role. Should fire via a Salesforce Flow or Process Builder action on the junction object, not as a manual task for the admin.

OTP login (no password)

The "Activate your account" CTA links to the Experience Cloud login page in passwordless email OTP mode. The user enters their email, receives a 6-digit code, and enters it. No password is ever created or required. This is the recommended pattern for new user activation as it removes the friction of password creation and reduces support load from forgotten passwords at first login.

Salesforce Experience Cloud has supported passwordless email OTP natively since Winter '23. It is enabled per-community in Setup → Digital Experiences → Settings → Login.

Role shown in email

The role shown is the role the admin assigned at approval time, which may differ from what the user requested. This helps set expectations before the user logs in and sees their access level.

Difference from PCP-APPROVE

PCP-APPROVE goes to the org admin (Jordan Reeves) with credentials and the onboarding checklist. PCP-REP-APPROVE goes to the individual rep (Alex Kim) with an activation link. The rep flow is lighter: OTP login → orientation → dashboard. No agreement signing, no setup wizard.

Personas

Recipient: Alex Kim (new CloudBridge hire, Deal Registration User). Approver who triggered this: Jordan Reeves (Portal Admin).

Connected pages

CTA links to onboarding-auth-setup.html (OTP login + auth setup). After activation, user lands in rep-first-login.html (first-login orientation wizard). Join request form at partner-user-request.html.
