Design Specs

Summary

Analytics dashboard for MDF spending visibility, ROI analysis by campaign category, partner participation rates, and budget forecasting with actionable recommendations. Featured data: CloudBridge Solutions 63% utilization ($47K of $75K annual allocation), $28K remaining for Q2.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Provides leadership visibility into MDF program ROI and spending effectiveness across activity types
  • Identifies highest-ROI campaign categories (Events 4.1x) to guide budget reallocation
  • Highlights partner engagement gaps by tier (Bronze 34% participation) for targeted enablement
  • Forecasts annual budget utilization (on pace for 85% EOY) to prevent over/under spending
  • Supports data-driven recommendations for budget optimization and program expansion decisions

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Five KPI cards: Total Allocated, Total Spent
  • Spending by Category horizontal bar chart showing Events & Sponsorships
  • Average ROI by Activity Type chart displaying Events, Webinars & Virtual
  • Partner Participation Rate by Tier
  • Top Performing Campaigns table
  • Underutilized Budget Alert noting Bronze partners significantly underutilizin...
  • Budget Status & Projections section showing Total Allocated, Spent YTD
  • Recommendations for Remaining Budget highlighting three strategic initiatives...

Interactive Elements

  • View Detailed Analytics button to drill into campaign performance metrics
  • Export Report button for download and stakeholder sharing
  • New Campaign Request button to initiate MDF request workflow
  • Table row hover states highlighting individual campaign records for selection

Functionality

  • MDF Spend Aggregation & Analysis
    • Aggregate MDF_Request__c records by activity category (Event, Digital Campaign, Content, etc.) and sum Approved_Amount__c
    • Calculate ROI per request by dividing Pipeline_Generated__c by Approved_Amount__c
    • Sort and rank campaigns by ROI, spend, and partner tier to identify top performers
  • Budget Utilization Tracking
    • Calculate quarterly budget utilization burn rate as (Actual Spend / Quarterly Allocation) × 100
    • Forecast EOY spend using linear projection from YTD spend and remaining quarter count
    • Compare forecast vs. annual budget and alert if projected overrun >5%
  • Partner Segmentation & Participation Analytics
    • Query Account records filtered by Partner_Tier__c (Gold, Silver, Bronze) and count active MDF requests
    • Calculate participation rate per tier as (Partners with MDF requests / Total partners in tier) × 100
    • Identify underutilized cohorts (Bronze tier <40% participation) and flag with alert recommendations
  • Campaign Performance Metrics
    • Calculate cost per lead by dividing campaign spend by leads generated from campaign tracking
    • Aggregate pipeline revenue generated from campaigns using Deal_Registration__c linked via Campaign_Source__c field
    • Display metrics by activity type and tier to enable comparative performance analysis
  • Reporting & Export
    • Generate exportable analytics report as PDF with spend trends, ROI rankings, and participation heatmaps
    • Include executive summary with KPIs and recommendations for MDF allocation optimization
    • Track report export timestamps for audit trail and stakeholder communication history
