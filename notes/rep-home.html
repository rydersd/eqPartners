Validation Rules & Edge Cases

Individual rep sees ONLY their own training/deals — No team visibility via SOQL filtering on Contact.Id AND Contact.AccountId. Query returns Contact where Id = LoggedInUserId AND ParentAccount.Type = 'Partner'.

Announcements filtered by tier + region — Display logic: Announcement.Target_Tier__c IN (Account.Partner_Tier__c, 'All Partners') AND Announcement.Display_Date__c <= TODAY() AND Announcement.Expiration__c >= TODAY().

Stats card caches on login, refreshes every 4 hours — Cache key: 'HOME_STATS_' + UserContactId. Automated process every 4 hours recalculates: COUNT(completed courses), COUNT(active certs), SUM(commission_amount) WHERE payment_status='Paid'.

Expired cert banner shows if any cert expired in last 30 days — Rule: Partner_Training__c WHERE Status='Completed' AND Has_Certification__c=true AND Expiration_Date__c < TODAY() AND Expiration_Date__c >= TODAY()-30.

Deal count shows deals in pipeline (Status NOT IN Closed Lost, Expired, Rejected) — Pipeline value = SUM(Amount) WHERE Status IN (Pending, Approved, Proposal, Negotiation, Closed Won).

Error States & Accessibility

Error: "Training data sync in progress — showing data as of [timestamp]" (displayed if Partner_Training__c last_modified_date > 30 mins ago).

Error: "No deals found" (empty state when Deal_Registration__c count = 0 for contact).

Accessibility: Completion badge has aria-label="4 of 8 courses completed, 50% progress". Deadline cards use role='region' with aria-live='polite'. Progress bars have aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin, aria-valuemax. Status conveyed by icon + color + text (never color alone).

Accessibility: Links to courses/certs have descriptive aria-labels. Activity list items are li elements within ul. Buttons have :focus states with 2px outline.

Primary User Flow: Deal Registration

The primary job-to-be-done for a partner rep is registering new deals and tracking their progress through the pipeline. Training is secondary.

1
Rep identifies an opportunity — A partner sales rep (Sarah) has a customer conversation that reveals a fit for Equinix services (colocation, Fabric, interconnection, etc.). She navigates to the Partner Portal and clicks "My Deals" or the quick-action card on Home.
2
Searches for existing deals — On the Register a Deal page, Sarah first uses the search panel to check if this customer or deal already exists in the system. The search queries Deal_Registration__c and Account records via fuzzy match. Results show confidence score (High/Medium/Low) and the registering rep.
3
Starts the 4-step wizard — If no match, Sarah clicks "No Match — Register New Deal." Step 1: Deal Basics (customer name with autocomplete against Salesforce Accounts, contact person, email, description). Step 2: Deal Details (estimated annual revenue, expected close date, product interest, deal source). Step 3: Competitive Info (primary competitor, their solution, Equinix differentiators). Step 4: Review & Submit with edit shortcuts back to each step.
4
Submits or saves as draft — Sarah can save at any point (Deal_Registration__c with Status='Draft'). On submission, she checks the terms agreement box and clicks "Submit for Approval." A Deal_Registration__c record is created with Status='Pending PSM Review'. Sarah receives an email confirmation.
5
PSM reviews within 2 business days — The assigned PSM (Marcus Chen) sees the deal in his queue (psm-deal-review-detail.html). He validates customer info, checks for conflicts with existing Equinix direct opportunities (AE bridge), and either approves, requests more info, or rejects. Approval triggers a Protection_Period__c record (90-day reservation).
6
Deal appears in rep's pipeline — Sarah navigates to "My Pipeline" (rep-pipeline.html) and sees the deal in the Kanban view under "Approved." She can track the protection period countdown (90 days), filter by stage/amount/time, and view her full deal table with last activity dates.
7
Deal progresses to close — As the customer moves through their buying process, the deal stage updates (Submitted → Under Review → Approved → Won/Lost). Sarah monitors from My Deals & Commission (rep-deals-commission.html) where she sees commission calculations: MRR × tier rate (Gold 15%, Silver 12%, Bronze 8%) + co-sell bonus (+2%).
8
Commission paid on customer invoice — When the deal closes (Status='Closed Won'), commission is calculated on MRR only (NRR excluded). Payment appears in rep's commission tracker with status (Pending → Approved → Paid). Quarterly payout via partner org's finance.

Primary User Flow: Deal Tracking & Pipeline Management

Once deals are registered, reps spend most of their portal time monitoring pipeline health and protection periods.

1
Rep logs in to check pipeline — Home dashboard shows deal stats at a glance (2 deals registered, $220K pipeline value, $10.2K commission YTD). Quick-action "My Deals" card provides one-click access.
2
Reviews Kanban pipeline view — rep-pipeline.html shows deals organized by stage (Submitted, Under Review, Approved, Won, Lost). Visual count per column lets Sarah assess pipeline health at a glance. Total pipeline value ($892K) displayed in header.
3
Checks protection period alerts — Yellow alert banner warns about deals with expiring protection (e.g., "CloudCorp Multi-Region has 12 days remaining"). Protection badges on each deal show green (safe, 30+ days), yellow (warning, 15-30 days), or red (critical, <15 days).
4
Filters and drills into details — Uses filter controls (stage, time period, amount range) to narrow view. Clicks a deal name to see full details, customer history, PSM notes, and next steps. Can request protection extension if deal is progressing but needs more time.
5
Monitors commission on won deals — Won deals transition to the "Won Deals → Orders" section showing order fulfillment status (Processing → Shipped → Delivered). Commission tab (rep-deals-commission.html) shows detailed breakdown per deal: MRR amount, tier rate, commission earned, and payment status.

Design Specs

Summary

Personalized dashboard for partner reps displaying certification progress toward tier goals, urgent deadlines and expiring items, quick-access action cards, activity log, and tier/PSM support information.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Display dashboard summary on portal login
  • Show urgent deadlines and overdue items prominently
  • Quick access to most common tasks
  • Monitor recent account activity and changes
  • See at-a-glance status of key metrics
  • Navigate to detailed pages from summary cards

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Welcome banner with greeting, tier status (e.g., "Gold Partner"), and certification progress badge (4/8 required)
  • Certification Progress hero card showing: count (4 of 8), percentage (50%), progress bar, estimated completion date
  • Three deadline cards with status-colored borders (Overdue: red, Expiring: amber, Upcoming: blue)
  • Deadline card details: title, remaining time countdown, progress bar, action button (e.g., "Take Now", "View Deal")
  • Quick Actions grid (2x2) with icon + title cards: Register Deal, Start Training, View Certifications, Messages
  • Recent Activity log with timeline dots, dates, actor names, action descriptions (8-10 items)
  • Announcements section with 2-3 alert boxes with left-border styling
  • Stats sidebar: Active Deals (3), Courses in Progress (2), Days Until Certification (45), YTD Commission ($12,500)
  • PSM Support card with assigned PSM name, title, contact info

Interactive Elements

  • Deadline cards clickable (navigate to detail page)
  • Quick action cards clickable (navigate to respective pages)
  • Activity item links (navigate to related records)
  • Recent Activity "View All" link
  • Stats sidebar stat items may link to detail pages
  • Announcement links for external resources or form submissions

Functionality

  • Welcome & Personalization
    • Welcome banner displays "Welcome, [Contact.FirstName]!" with Account.Partner_Tier__c badge (Bronze|Silver|Gold)
    • Certification Progress displayed in banner as percentage: (COUNT(Certification__c WHERE Contact=User.ContactId AND Status='Active') / Tier_Cert_Requirement__mdt[Account.Partner_Tier__c].Required_Count) × 100
    • Personalized content zones loaded based on User role, Account.Partner_Tier__c, and previous page visits (stored in session)
  • Deadline & Urgency Cards
    • Deadline cards query highest-priority items: overdue Training_Assignment__c (Status='Not Started' AND Due_Date__c < TODAY()), expiring Certification__c (Status='Active' AND Expiration_Date__c < TODAY()+30), next Opportunity close date (StageName='Proposal' AND CloseDate within 7 days)
    • Countdown timer calculated as DAYS(Due_Date__c, TODAY()); displayed in amber if negative (overdue) or red if <= 3 days
    • Cards sorted by urgency (most overdue first); limited to 3-5 top items on homepage
  • Quick Actions & Navigation
    • Quick Actions are hardcoded navigation links with icons (Browse Courses, View Deals, My Training, Certifications) that route to respective portal pages
    • Cards are styled consistently and clickable; onClick redirects to target page
  • Recent Activity Feed
    • Recent Activity populated from ActivityHistory, Task, and Event records; SOQL query limited to 8-10 most recent (CreatedDate DESC)
    • Each activity item displays: actor name (CreatedBy.Name), action description (Subject for Task/Event), timestamp (CreatedDate formatted as "X days ago")
    • Activity items clickable; links navigate to related records (Opportunity detail, Training, etc.)
  • Announcements & Communications
    • Announcements fetched from Portal_Announcement__c WHERE IsActive__c=true, ordered by PostDate__c DESC; limited to 3 displayed on homepage
    • Each announcement includes Title__c, Body__c, Icon_URL__c, and optional Link__c; truncated to preview length with "Read More" link
  • Dashboard Statistics & Support
    • Stats sidebar calculates: Active Deals = COUNT(Opportunity WHERE StageName != 'Closed Won' AND Account=Contact.Account__c), In Progress Training = COUNT(Partner_Training__c WHERE Contact=User.ContactId AND Status='In Progress'), YTD Commission = SUM(Commission_Tracking__c.Commission_Amount__c WHERE YEAR(Payment_Date__c)=YEAR(TODAY())), Days to Next Cert = MIN(days remaining for next certification requirement)
    • PSM Support card fetches Account.PSM__c relationship; displays PSM name, title, email, phone; clicking card navigates to PSM profile or initiates contact action

Technical Details

Salesforce Queries & Logic

  • Welcome: Contact first name, Account.Partner_Tier__c, Certification progress = COUNT(Certification__c) where Contact=User.ContactId and Status='Active'
  • Certification Progress percentage: (active cert count / tier requirement) * 100; tier requirement fetched from Tier_Cert_Requirement__mdt
  • Deadline cards: highest-priority items from Training_Assignment__c (overdue), Certification__c (expiring soon), Opportunity (closing soon)
  • Recent Activity: Query ActivityHistory, Task, Event limited to 10 records, ordered by CreatedDate DESC
  • Announcements: Portal_Announcement__c WHERE IsActive__c = true, ordered by PostDate__c DESC, limited to 3
  • Stats: COUNT(Opportunity) where Account = current contact's account and StageName != 'Closed Won', COUNT(Partner_Training__c) where Status='In Progress', Days to certification = MIN(Certification path completion date) - TODAY(), YTD Commission = SUM(Commission_Amount__c) from Commission_Tracking__c
