Design Notes

Summary

Lead Referral Partner Application — separate registration path for partners who refer opportunities (vs. resellers). Entered from onboarding-partner-type.html after selecting Lead Referral. Step 0 confirms the type selection and links back to the type guide if the user wants to change. Steps 1–4 are: Company & You → Your Markets → Your Business (referral-specific) → Review & Submit.

Flow Position

  • Entry: onboarding-partner-type.html (user selected Lead Referral and clicked Continue)
  • On submit: → onboarding-agreement-confirmation.html
  • Sibling form: onboarding-apply-form.html (Reseller path)
  • Auth required: No — public / unauthenticated

Step 3 — Lead Referral–Specific Fields

  • Business type: IT Reseller/VAR, Independent Consultant, ISV, MSP, Systems Integrator, Telecom Agent/Broker, Technology Alliance Partner, Other
  • Years in business: Less than 1 year → 20+ years
  • Lead generation methods: Checkboxes (multi-select) — 5 options
  • Typical deal value referred: Radio — Under $25K / $25K–$100K / $100K–$500K / $500K+
  • Estimated annual referrals: Radio — 1–5 / 6–15 / 16–50 / 50+
  • Existing Equinix customer: Yes / No
  • Existing channel relationship: Yes with PSM / Prior engagement / No
  • Notes: Optional free-text

Design Notes

  • No type gate — type is confirmed (pre-selected) at Step 0. "Change type" link goes back to onboarding-partner-type.html.
  • Shorter than reseller form — no revenue history, employee counts, or business mix. ~5 minutes estimated.
  • Step 3 fields are referral-specific; Steps 1–2 and 4 are identical in structure to the reseller form.
  • Review step shows partner type as a static badge (not editable — user must go to Step 0 via Edit link to change).
  • Both paths (this form + onboarding-apply-form.html) submit to onboarding-agreement-confirmation.html.
