PSM Observations & Partner Response

Marcus Chen's Observations (PSM):

CloudBridge is showing strong revenue momentum — the Meridian Capital deal (post-escalation resolution) closed in February and contributed $420K to Q1 revenue. The pipeline is solid with 3 additional deals in final negotiation stage for Q2.

The certification gap is the primary concern. Two Gold-tier certifications lapsed in January (Steve Rogers, Security Fundamentals + Network Architecture). Renewal courses are enrolled but not completed. If not resolved by June 30, CloudBridge risks non-compliance with the PPA certification requirement — this is a compliance blocker that needs immediate escalation.

MDF non-utilization in Q1 is unusual for CloudBridge — Jordan indicated the planned co-marketing campaign was delayed due to internal restructuring. Q2 plan should include a make-good activity to utilize the Q1 carry-forward allocation ($18.75K). New logos pipeline is also light; recommend co-sell support to accelerate Nexus Global close and identify 1-2 additional opportunities.

Recommended action: Schedule a mid-quarter call with Jordan and Brian Walsh (VP) to align on cert renewal priority and confirm Q2 MDF plan.

Design Note

Check-in records are stored as JBP_Checkin__c child records on JBP__c. PSM creates the check-in, enters observations, and submits. Partner receives an in-portal notification and can add their response. Both parties sign off. Brian Walsh (VP) receives an auto-generated summary email on completion.

The "Export Check-in PDF" generates a one-page exec summary using Salesforce Document Builder template (TMPL-JBP-CHECKIN-SUMMARY) — not Agentforce DocGen, which is reserved for escalation briefs.
