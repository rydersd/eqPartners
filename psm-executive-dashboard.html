Design Specs

Summary

Executive-level dashboard providing channel program health metrics, regional performance comparisons, and revenue trend analysis for strategic decision-making.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Provide C-level view of channel program health and revenue drivers
  • Compare regional performance and identify growth leaders
  • Highlight top partners and potential churn risks
  • Support strategic decision-making on channel investments
  • Track progress toward annual channel revenue targets

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Six KPI cards: Channel Revenue, % of Total Revenue
  • Regional Performance Summary grid
  • Channel Revenue Trend timeline
  • Top 10 Partners by Revenue table
  • Key Insights cards: AMER Region Leading Growth, Partner Tier Quality Gap

Interactive Elements

  • Export Summary Report button
  • View Analytics button
  • Schedule Reviews button

Functionality

  • Revenue Aggregation & KPI Calculation
    • Aggregate annual revenue from all approved Deal_Registration__c records using SOQL sum functions
    • Calculate channel revenue as percentage of total company revenue by dividing Deal total by TCV from Opportunity pipeline
    • Count new partner additions by filtering Account records created in current fiscal quarter
    • Compute partner churn rate as (Partners lost / Total start-of-period partners) × 100
  • Regional Performance Analytics
    • Calculate regional revenue splits from Deal_Registration__c records aggregated by BillingCountry__c field
    • Map countries to regions (AMER, EMEA, APAC, LATAM) using custom region mapping object
    • Count active partners per region from Account records with region = geography
    • Calculate YoY growth by comparing current period revenue against prior-year Deal__c total
  • Partner Ranking & Health Scoring
    • Identify top-10 partners by total revenue using sorted Deal_Registration__c rollup by Account
    • Determine tier status from Account_Partner_Tier__c lookup relationship
    • Display health score from calculated Partner_Health_Score__c metric based on engagement, revenue, compliance
    • Rank by revenue contribution and tier classification (Gold, Silver, Bronze)
  • Risk Detection & Alerting
    • Trigger churn-risk alerts for partners with declining ACV (comparing current deal value vs prior period avg)
    • Identify engagement warnings for partners with no activity in last 90 days (e.g., no deals, no pipeline)
    • Flag Silver-tier partners scoring below 65 health for proactive QBR scheduling
  • Reporting & Export
    • Generate exportable summary report as PDF or Excel using Salesforce rendering engine and Deal + Account data
    • Include charts for revenue trend, regional breakdown, and partner tier distribution
    • Export timestamp-labeled file for audit trail and historical tracking
