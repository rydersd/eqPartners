Design Notes

Summary

Step 3 of the Deal Registration wizard. Jordan Reeves identifies the primary sales contact for the deal. A "I am the primary contact" checkbox auto-fills the form from the logged-in user's profile. Manual entry covers first/last name, job title, partner company, email, phone, and Equinix Partner ID.

Flow Position

  • Previous: pc-dr-step2.html (Deal Details)
  • Next: pc-dr-step4.html (Review)

Design Decisions

  • Self-contact shortcut — The "I am the primary contact" checkbox pre-fills all fields from the authenticated user's profile, reducing friction for the common case where the person registering the deal is also the contact. Fields become visually muted/read-only when pre-filled but can still be overridden.
  • Equinix Partner ID — Optional but important for attribution. Hint text points users to where to find it.
  • AI rail — Will surface contact lookup and Partner ID validation in a future release.

Form Fields

  • Self-contact checkbox — optional; auto-fills form from user profile
  • First Name — required
  • Last Name — required
  • Job Title — required
  • Partner Company — required
  • Email Address — required
  • Phone Number — optional
  • Equinix Partner ID — optional
