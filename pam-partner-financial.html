PAM Portal · Alex Rivera · Partner Financial Detail

Job to Be Done

"When reviewing a partner's financial standing, I want a single view of MDF utilization, rebates, revenue vs. tier threshold, and compliance status — so I can act before the review window closes."

Screen Purpose

The per-partner financial record for Alex. Everything needed to manage one partner's financial lifecycle in one place: who they are, how they're tracking against tier thresholds, what MDF they've spent and have pending, their rebate status, and whether their compliance docs are current.

Key Design Decisions

  • Alert banner for at-risk partners — triggered when revenue gap to tier threshold and low MDF utilization both present simultaneously
  • Tab-based layout — default tab is MDF & Spend; Revenue and Rebates tabs reduce cognitive load for the common case
  • Progress bars for budget utilization — visual encoding of MDF spend rate is much faster to read than a table of numbers
  • Compliance status in left rail — always visible regardless of which financial tab is active; compliance gates every financial action
  • PAM-owner callout — explicitly shows Alex as the financial owner, Marcus as the PSM; reinforces the PAM/PSM role split

Data Model

  • Account: Partner_Tier__c, Tier_Since__c, PAM__c, PSM__c, Partner_Type__c
  • MDF_Allocation__c: Partner_Account__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Allocated_Amount__c, Spent_Amount__c, Pending_Amount__c
  • MDF_Activity__c: Allocation__c, Activity_Type__c, Requested_Date__c, Approved_Date__c, Approved_Amount__c, Claimed_Amount__c, Status__c, ROI__c
  • Rebate__c: Partner_Account__c, Rebate_Type__c, Amount__c, Paid_Date__c
  • Partner_Revenue__c: Partner_Account__c, Fiscal_Year__c, Total_ARR__c (rolled up from Opportunities)
  • Compliance_Doc__c: Partner_Account__c, Doc_Type__c, Status__c, Expiry_Date__c
