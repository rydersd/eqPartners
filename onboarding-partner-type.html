Partner Type Guide

Choose the partner type that matches how you work with customers. The main difference is whether you refer opportunities for Equinix to sell directly, or resell Equinix services as part of what you offer. Not sure which one fits? Use the quick descriptions below — then scroll down to compare the two types side by side. If your motion is mixed, choose the one you'll use most often.

Select your partner type
Lead Referral Partner

You refer. Equinix sells.

You introduce qualified opportunities. Equinix owns the contract, billing, and delivery. Lighter setup — right for consultants, agents, and influence-led motions.

Reseller

You sell Equinix services.

You own the commercial relationship and may bundle Equinix into your offerings. More setup required — right for VARs, MSPs, and solution-led motions.

How the two types compare
Decision question Lead Referral Partner Reseller
Do you sign the customer contract for Equinix services? No — Equinix contracts directly Yes / Often — you transact (varies by model)
Do you invoice the customer for Equinix services? No Yes / Often (or bundled into your invoice)
Who owns the customer commercially? Equinix You
What are you primarily doing? Introducing opportunities Selling + packaging services
Do you bundle Equinix with your own services? Sometimes, but typically influence-only Common (managed services / solution bundles)
Who runs quoting + order placement? Equinix You (or you initiate and manage the process)
Who provides first-line customer support? Equinix You (with Equinix escalation support)
What do you need to be set up for? Lead submission + tracking Commercial model + quoting / ordering + operational requirements
Best fit if… You want to refer and stay lightweight You want to sell and own the relationship
Quick picker rule 1 If you don't contract and don't bill the customer → Lead Referral Partner. If you do contract and/or bill → Reseller.
Quick picker rule 2 If your motion is mixed, choose your primary model. Your PSM can adjust your setup during onboarding if needed.

Design Notes

Summary

Partner Type Guide — pre-registration landing page. This is the entry to both registration paths. User selects a type here; Continue routes them to the appropriate form. No wizard chrome or stepper on this page.

Flow Position

  • Entry path A: equinix.com → "Become a Partner" CTA → this page. User is unauthenticated; came directly from the public site.
  • Entry path B: Partner Central login page → "Create Account" link → this page. User attempted to log in, doesn't have an account, and chose to create one.
  • Both paths land here: No account exists yet in either case. Account creation happens downstream as part of the apply flow.
  • Re-entry: Nav ("Partner Org Registration") and "← Change type" links from both apply forms
  • Exit — Lead Referral: Continue → onboarding-apply-referral.html
  • Exit — Reseller: Continue → onboarding-apply-form.html
  • Auth required: No — public / unauthenticated. Account is created as part of the downstream apply flow.

Interaction

  • Type cards are interactive radio buttons (ARIA role="radio", keyboard accessible)
  • Continue button is disabled on load; enabled once a card is selected
  • Hint text below button clears on selection
  • Continue routes to the form for the selected type

Design Notes

  • Cards are above the comparison matrix — user chooses first, compares second if needed
  • Matrix covers 9 key decision questions plus 2 quick-picker rules (highlighted rows)
  • Both forms share the same confirmation flow on submit
