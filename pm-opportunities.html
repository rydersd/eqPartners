Validation Rules & Business Logic
  • Partners see only their own opportunities and orders (row-level security via Org)
  • Equinix AE field is required for all opportunities in Qualification stage or higher
  • Next Action field is mandatory and must be updated within 7 days of last activity
  • Protection Period countdown triggered when Opportunity reaches Proposal stage
  • Commission status defaults to Pending until Equinix Finance approves Order
  • Deal must reach Closed Won stage before Order creation is allowed
  • Delivery milestones auto-created from Order template when status = "Closed Won"
Error States
  • "Equinix AE required — cannot move to Qualification without AE assignment"
  • "Protection period expiring in 7 days — escalate or update Next Action"
  • "No active opportunities — register your first deal from the Deals page"
  • "Commission on hold — Order pending Equinix finance approval"
  • "Delivery milestone overdue — update status or extend expected date"
  • "Next Action stale — last activity >7 days ago, update to re-engage"
Accessibility Notes
  • Stage flow diagram uses aria-label to describe progression: "Deal flow from registration through 12 opportunities, 10 qualified, 8 proposals, 5 quotes, 3 closing orders"
  • All stage badges include both color and text label (e.g., "Negotiation" in teal, not color-only)
  • Protection period warning uses color (var(--wf-amber, #8b7745)) + bold text, not color-alone; screen readers announce "7 days warning"
  • Commission status badges use aria-label: "Commission status: Paid" or "Pending review"
  • Opportunity and Order tables marked with role="table" and aria-label; keyboard navigation via Tab + arrow keys supported
  • Next Action text is conversational and readable (e.g., "Send proposal by Feb 20" vs. "PROP_SEND_2_20")
  • Your Rep and Equinix AE columns show human names with context (Partner vs. Equinix)
User Flow (Numbered Steps)
  1. PM logs in and lands on Opportunities & Orders page
  2. PM reviews KPI cards: Total Pipeline ($4.2M), Avg Deal Size ($525K), Win Rate (62%), Deals Closing (3)
  3. PM checks Stage Flow diagram to understand deal progression (12 registered → 10 qualified → 8 proposals → 5 quotes → 3 orders)
  4. PM applies filters (by Stage, Your Rep, Equinix AE, Date Range) to segment pipeline
  5. PM scans Active Opportunities table for high-risk deals (protection period <14 days highlighted in orange)
  6. PM identifies next actions needed by stage: Prospecting→discovery call, Qualification→technical review, Proposal→send/review, Negotiation→await sign-off
  7. PM reviews Equinix AE column to see which Equinix rep is co-selling (cross-functional visibility)
  8. PM clicks on opportunity name to view details or update stage
  9. PM scrolls to Won Orders section to track closed deals and commission status
  10. PM reviews delivery milestones for key orders to track post-sale progress
  11. PM uses commission status badges to identify which orders have received payment
  12. PM returns to Deals page to register new opportunities or updates Active Opportunities in real-time

Design Brief — Partner-Centric Opportunities & Orders
This page shifts focus from partner-to-Equinix deal tracking (Deals page) to partner-to-customer opportunity management with Equinix co-sell visibility. Partners need three critical views:

1. Pipeline at a Glance: Four KPI cards show total pipeline value, average deal size, win rate, and near-term closing forecasts. A horizontal stage flow diagram visualizes the funnel (12 regs → 10 opps → 8 proposals → 5 quotes → 3 closing), helping partners understand velocity and conversion rates.

2. Active Opportunities Table: Eight deals span all stages. Key columns include Your Rep (the partner-side rep driving the deal), Equinix AE (the Equinix Account Executive co-selling), and Next Action (conversational, time-bound tasks like "Send proposal by Feb 20" or "Schedule technical review"). This structure ensures partners know who at Equinix is engaged and what must happen next. Protection Period countdown (with <14 day warning in orange) flags deals at risk of losing special discount window. Amount and Stage badges (with color + text) show deal size and progression status at a glance.

3. Won Orders & Delivery Tracking: Five closed orders show post-sale progress. Commission Status badges (Paid/Approved/Pending) indicate which deals have earned partner revenue. Delivery milestones for a selected order show infrastructure provisioning progress, helping partners understand service delivery timelines and customer onboarding status.

Filtering & Segmentation: Partners can filter by Stage, Your Rep, Equinix AE, and Date Range to isolate deals needing attention (e.g., "all Negotiation-stage deals co-sold by Sarah Mitchell").

Cross-Functional Transparency: By showing Equinix AE names and co-sell status, the page reinforces partner-Equinix alignment and encourages collaborative deal execution. Partners can quickly identify stalled deals or escalation needs.
Architectural Decision: MRR-Only Revenue Model

All Amount values on this page represent Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) — the contracted monthly value of colocation, cross-connects, and managed services. Non-Recurring Revenue (NRR) such as installation fees, smart hands, and custom buildouts is excluded from opportunity pipeline values and commission calculations.

This aligns partner incentives with Equinix's recurring revenue model. Partners are motivated to close larger, longer-term service contracts rather than one-time setup work. Commission rates (Bronze 8%, Silver 12%, Gold 15%) apply to MRR only.

  • Understand pipeline size and deal progression across all stages
  • Identify deals at risk (protection period expiring, stalled, overdue action)

  • Track won orders and commission approval status for closed deals

  • Ensure next actions are defined and updated regularly for each opportunity

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • KPI cards: Total Pipeline Value, Average Deal Size, Deals in Proposal, Orders Pending Commission
  • Deal Flow & Stage Progression diagram with visual stage bands
  • Active Opportunities table with stage, customer, amount, close date, protection status
  • Won Orders table with order number, deal link, commission status, approval date
  • Delivery Milestones section showing critical post-sale events and timelines
  • Filter controls for Stage, Your Rep, Equinix AE, date range

Interactive Elements

  • Stage flow diagram with colored circles showing deal progression and counts
  • Filter dropdowns for Stage, Your Rep, Equinix AE, and date range
  • Opportunity names in table are clickable links to detail views and next actions
  • Order numbers in Won Orders table are clickable to view order records and commission detail
  • Protection period warning badges with expiration countdown
  • Commission status badges (Pending, Approved, Paid) with color coding

Functionality

  • Access Control & Validation
    • Row-level security filter ensures partners see only opportunities where they are Owner or Equinix AE
    • Opportunity stage validation requires Equinix AE assignment once opportunity reaches Qualification stage or higher
  • Action Tracking & Monitoring
    • Next Action mandatory field with 7-day staleness detection and alert to keep deals moving forward
    • Stale opportunity detection flags deals with no activity for 30+ days with visual warning indicators
  • Protection & Commission Lifecycle
    • Protection Period countdown triggered upon Proposal stage entry; automatically expires at 90-day mark with notification
    • Commission status workflow: Pending (on Order creation) → Approved (Finance approval) → Paid (GL posting) with status tracking
