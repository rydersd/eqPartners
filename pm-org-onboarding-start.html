Apply to Join the Equinix Partner Channel Program

Complete this 5-step application to register your organization. Once reviewed and approved, you'll gain access to the Partner Portal, deal registration, MDF co-marketing funds, and commission tracking.

Application Overview
1

Company Profile

Legal entity information, business address, and authorized signatory.

2

Tax & Banking

Tax classification form (W-9 or W-8BEN-E) and ACH banking details for commission payments.

3

Partnership Agreement

Review and e-sign the Master Partner Agreement and Program Terms & Conditions.

4

Team Setup

Designate your Partner Manager, technical contact, and select your product tracks.

5

Review & Submit

Confirm your application details and submit for Equinix review.

Gather these before you begin

  • Legal business name and country of incorporation
  • Tax ID number (US: EIN; International: local registration number)
  • W-9 (US entities) or W-8BEN-E (non-US entities)
  • Bank account details for commission payments (ACH routing + account number)
  • Authorized signatory name, title, and email
  • VAT/GST registration number (EU/UK/India entities only)
Typical timeline: 3–5 business days for review after all documents are received. You'll get a reference number immediately after submission.
