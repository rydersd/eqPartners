Design Specs

Summary

Visualizes partner lifecycle stages from initial prospect through onboarded status. Featured: Peter Parker (peter.parker@stark.com), Junior Sales Rep at Stark Industries, joined Jan 30, 2026, in early-stage onboarding (20% complete, BIZ track, target Apr 30). PSMs use this Kanban board to monitor onboarding progress, identify stalled applications, and manage stage transitions through required checklist items.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Tracks full partner lifecycle from first contact to active partner with status badges
  • Each stage has required checklist items (8-item master checklist)
  • Stalled detection: cards turn red border after 14 days in same stage
  • Stage advancement (auto or manual) triggers automated stage-based emails and Portal provisioning
  • Completed onboarding (all checklist items done) rolls partner into active Account with Bronze tier default
  • Partnership types: Reseller, Referral, Technology Partner, Systems Integrator (SI)

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Five Kanban board columns: Prospect, Application Review, Agreement Signed, Portal Setup, Onboarded
  • KPI header cards displaying key metrics (Active Prospects, Applications This Month, Stalled Count)
  • Partner cards per column showing company name, size, contact person, PSM assigned, partnership type
  • Expanded detail card for selected onboarding showing stage-specific checklist and next steps
  • Stalled Onboardings alert section highlighting applications > 14 days in current stage

Interactive Elements

  • Drag and drop partner cards between columns to advance stage
  • Details button to expand card and view checklist items and required actions
  • Send Reminder button on stalled cards to notify partner manager
  • Filter controls by Partnership Type and Region
  • New Partner Application button to initiate new onboarding
  • Bulk Send Reminder action on stalled section
  • Assign Training Plan, View Portal Credentials, and Send Reminder buttons in expanded view

Functionality

  • Kanban State Management
    • Reads Partner_Onboarding__c.Stage__c field tracking five stages: Prospect, Application Review, Agreement Signed, Portal Setup, Onboarded
    • Updates Stage__c and Stage_Entry_Date__c when partner card is dragged between columns, triggering stage-specific workflows
  • Stalled Onboarding Detection
    • Calculates stalled status via formula: Stage_Entry_Date__c + 14 days < TODAY() triggers red border styling on partner card
    • Alerts aggregates all stalled onboardings and displays "Send Reminder" bulk action to notify PSMs of aged applications
  • Checklist Validation & Stage Blocking
    • Validation rule prevents stage advancement if required checklist items incomplete (e.g., "NDA must be signed before Application Review")
    • Expanded detail card displays 8-item master checklist with checkboxes; stage transition blocked until all prerequisites marked complete
  • Portal Provisioning & Completion
    • Portal_Setup stage entry triggers automated workflow: creates Partner Portal user account via Salesforce Partner Community API and generates credential email
    • Onboarded stage completion converts Partner_Onboarding__c record to Account object with IsPartner__c = true, Partner_Tier__c = 'Bronze', sets Active_Status__c = 'Active'
  • Auto-Rejection & Email Workflows
    • Scheduled batch job evaluates Prospect stage entries with no activity (Task/Event CreatedDate) for 30 days, auto-marks as Rejected_Status__c = true
    • Stage transitions trigger Salesforce Flow to send predefined email templates to Partner_Manager__c contact and internal PSM team via email alert
  • Duplicate Prevention
    • Before stage advance, system queries Account and Partner_Onboarding__c records matching submitted Account Name; displays warning modal if duplicate found
