Design Specs

Summary

Health scorecard for a single partner account, aggregating engagement, revenue, and performance metrics with trend analysis and actionable intervention recommendations.

Jobs to Be Done

  • Provide comprehensive health assessment of individual partner at a glance
  • Identify partnership risks and areas needing intervention before churn occurs
  • Track partner trajectory over time with quarterly trend arrows
  • Translate data into actionable recommendations for PSM engagement
  • Support QBR preparation with data-driven conversation starters

Design Specification

Primary Content

  • Six KPI cards: Revenue vs Target, Training Completion
  • Health Indicators by Categoryrows with color dots for Revenue Performance
  • Trend Analysis sectionquarterly trend visualizations for Revenue, Training
  • Overall Partnership Health Score/100, Yellow status
  • Action Recommendationscards with titles and detailed text

Interactive Elements

  • Schedule Health Review Meeting button
  • Export as PDF button
  • View Full Trends button

Functionality

  • Revenue Aggregation & Performance Tracking
    • Aggregate quarterly revenue from Account's Opportunity rollups filtered by status = Closed/Won
    • Compare current period revenue against target from Account_Target_Revenue__c field to display % attainment
    • Calculate YTD revenue by summing all Closed/Won Opportunities from January through current month
  • Multi-Dimensional Health Scoring
    • Score health indicators (revenue, engagement, MDF spend, training certification, deal velocity) on 0-100 scale
    • Weight component scores: Revenue 30%, Training 25%, MDF Utilization 20%, Deal Activity 15%, Certification Coverage 10%
    • Calculate overall health as weighted average and display status badge (Green 75+, Yellow 50-75, Red <50)
  • Trend Analysis & Historical Benchmarking
    • Generate quarterly trend data by querying Partner_Health_Score_History__c for last 4 quarters
    • Compare current quarter to prior quarter baseline to display trend arrows (↑ improving, → stable, ↓ declining)
    • Benchmark partner against tier cohort average from Account_Partner_Tier__c lookup
  • Risk Detection & AI Recommendations
    • Flag low-scoring categories (score < 60) and trigger alert recommendations for intervention
    • Detect churn signals by analyzing declining trends, low engagement scores, and 90+ day inactivity
    • Generate AI-recommended action items with priority levels based on health score impact and urgency
  • Engagement & Export Functionality
    • Schedule Health Review meeting by creating Task and CalendarEvent records linked to Account
    • Export scorecard as PDF via Salesforce rendering engine with all metrics, trends, and recommendations
    • Render historical QBR artifacts and prior scorecard versions for comparison
